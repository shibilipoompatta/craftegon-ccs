Track 1 • Direct Corporate Secretarial Advisory (CCS) Companies Act, 2013

Procedural Guidance & Secretarial Filings

For companies and boards navigating director removal, resignation, or boardroom deadlocks, CraftEgon CCS assists with statutory secretarial compliance and documentation protocols:

  • Special Notice Review: Verification and drafting of Special Notice under Section 115 from eligible shareholders.
  • Board & EGM Convening: Drafting board meeting notices, agendas, director intimations under Section 169(3), and EGM notices under Section 100/101 with Section 102 Explanatory Statements.
  • Right of Representation: Formulating procedures for the concerned director's written representation and circulation under Section 169(4).
  • RoC Statutory E-Filing: Preparation and electronic filing of Form DIR-12 (cessation/removal) and Form MGT-14 (resolutions) with the Registrar of Companies.
  • Statutory Registers: Updating the Register of Directors and Key Managerial Personnel under Section 170.

Track 2 • Contentious Tribunal Litigation (Independent Counsel) Advocates Act, 1961

Tribunal Advocacy & Injunction Defense

Where a director removal or boardroom dispute escalates into contested litigation before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), civil courts, or appellate benches, representation must be conducted by independent legal counsel:

  • Tribunal Pleadings: Drafting company petitions, applications for interim injunctions under Section 242(4), and caveator filings must be signed and filed by practicing advocates holding a valid vakalatnama.
  • Court Appearances: Oral arguments and appearances before NCLT benches cannot be conducted by corporate advisory entities.
  • Client Selection: External representation must be retained independently through qualified legal counsel of the client's choice. CraftEgon CCS maintains no in-house litigation advocacy service, exclusive advocate referral agreements, or legal fee sharing.
Documentation Bundle for Independent Counsel: CraftEgon CCS can assist the company in assembling certified secretarial records for their retained advocate, including certified Articles of Association, signed board minutes, proof of notice dispatch, and MCA Master Data filings.
