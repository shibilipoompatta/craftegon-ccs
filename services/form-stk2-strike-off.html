Form STK-2 Company Strike-Off & Voluntary Closure

Enables defunct or inoperative corporate entities to voluntarily dissolve their corporate shell and remove their name from the ROC register cleanly without prolonged court winding-up.

Statutory Authority

Section 248(2), Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies (Removal of Names of Companies from the Register of Companies) Rules, 2016

Governing Jurisdiction

Republic of India (Central Portal)

Execution SLA

3 to 5 Business Days

Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Enables defunct or inoperative corporate entities to voluntarily dissolve their corporate shell and remove their name from the ROC register cleanly without prolonged court winding-up.

Statutory Mandate: Section 248(2), Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies (Removal of Names of Companies from the Register of Companies) Rules, 2016

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Companies that have failed to commence business within 1 year of incorporation, or have not carried on business for 2 consecutive financial years without applying for dormant status.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Zero assets and zero liabilities statement certified by CA in Form STK-8 (< 30 days old)
  • Form STK-3 (Indemnity Bond) executed by all directors on requisite state stamp paper
  • Form STK-4 (Affidavit) executed by all directors on state stamp paper and notarized
  • Certified true copy of Special Resolution passed by 75% shareholders in terms of paid-up capital
  • Bank account closure certificate from all corporate commercial bankers
  • Indemnity against any future tax/regulatory liabilities

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Audit books; extinguish all liabilities, commercial debts, and close corporate bank accounts
  2. Step 2: Convene EGM and pass special resolution with 75% shareholder approval; file Form MGT-14
  3. Step 3: Obtain CA certified statement of accounts in Form STK-8; execute STK-3 indemnity and STK-4 affidavits
  4. Step 4: Launch Form STK-2 on MCA V3; attach STK-8, STK-3, STK-4, special resolution, and bank closure letters
  5. Step 5: Affix Director and certifying professional DSCs (CA/CS); submit with ₹10,000 statutory fee
  6. Step 6: ROC publishes public notice in Form STK-6; upon 30 days with no objection, ROC issues STK-7 dissolution notice

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Applying with existing liabilities or fraudulent concealment subjects directors to personal liability for all claims under Section 249/251 and criminal fraud prosecution under Section 447 (6 months to 10 years imprisonment + fine).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a company with pending litigation or tax demands file Form STK-2?
No. All outstanding tax demands, government dues, and pending litigation must be settled before filing Form STK-2.
How long does it take for a company to be struck off after filing STK-2?
Typically 60 to 90 days following public notice publication in the official gazette and newspapers.
