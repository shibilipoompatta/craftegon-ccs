Complete MCA V3 Portal E-Filing & User Registration Guide

A comprehensive walkthrough of user role management, business user profiling, and web-form execution protocols on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs V3 platform.

1. Understanding the MCA V3 Architecture

The MCA V3 system transitions corporate filings from downloadable PDF-based e-forms to cloud-enabled web forms. Users must create distinct profiles depending on their operational capacity:

  • Registered User (RU): For general stakeholders, investors, and corporate employees submitting routine requests.
  • Business User (BU): Tailored for directors, managers, and authorized signatories requiring company-linked filing rights.
  • Professional User (PU): Dedicated portal access for Practicing Company Secretaries (PCS), Chartered Accountants (CA), and Cost Accountants (CMA) for certification and submission.

2. Step-by-Step E-Filing Workflow

  1. Profile Association: Map your DIN or professional membership credentials to your V3 login profile using OTP and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) validation.
  2. Web-Form Initiation: Select the required service under My Applications (e.g., Annual Filings, Event-based changes, or Incorporation forms). Data is saved dynamically on the cloud as you progress.
  3. Professional Certification: Where mandated under the Companies Act, attach professional digital signatures (DSC) for form validation.
  4. Payment Gateway & SRN Generation: Complete fee remittances via the secure MCA payment gateway to generate a Service Request Number (SRN) for tracking.
