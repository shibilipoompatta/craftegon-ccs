Step 1 • Kerala Market Reality

Why Standard Registration Fails After Day 1 in Kerala

Kerala Demand Dynamics

High-Intent Capital & Sector Expansion

Kerala's premium experiential tourism and wellness hospitality sector attracts major NRI and institutional capital, creating strong demand for compliant SPV structuring and end-to-end pre-opening regulatory governance.

Where Fragmented Vendors Fail

The Post-Registration Operational Stall

Resort promoters frequently lock into property leases or construction before catching LSGD commercial building conversion requirements, KSPCB Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) consent thresholds, CRZ/eco-zone checks, Fire NOC rules, and unenforceable operator/OTA/contractor terms.

Why does Hospitality, Boutique Stays & Eco/Wellness Resorts need a Launch-to-Operate bundle instead of just entity incorporation? +

Our bundled roadmap coordinates entity formation alongside Kerala pre-launch licensing dossiers, sector-specific CraftEgon LLP contracts, and Year-1 statutory cadence.

Are government fees, stamp duty, and statutory board charges included in CCS advisory fees? +

Sub-Registrar Stamp Duty, Panchayat building/trade fees, KSPCB/Fire/Tourism levies, and FSSAI fees are billed strictly at actuals. You receive an itemised scope separation before work begins.

Does CraftEgon guarantee statutory approval timelines from K-SWIFT, LSGD, or regulatory boards? +

Resort building occupancy, CRZ/environmental clearances, KSPCB consents, and Tourism classifications are governed by physical site conditions and competent statutory authorities with no fixed clearance timelines or assured approval outcomes.
