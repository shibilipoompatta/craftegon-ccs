Statutory Notice & Scope of Retainers: CraftEgon CCS subscription retainers cover routine corporate secretarial compliance, statutory register maintenance, ROC filing staging, and secretarial documentation under the Companies Act, 2013 and LLP Act, 2008. In compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961, retainers do not include courtroom appearances, tribunal advocacy, or contentious litigation representation before the NCLT, ITAT, or High Courts. Contentious matters and tribunal representation must be handled by independent practicing advocates directly engaged on a separate vakalatnama. Government filing fees and stamp duties are statutory disbursements charged at actuals.
