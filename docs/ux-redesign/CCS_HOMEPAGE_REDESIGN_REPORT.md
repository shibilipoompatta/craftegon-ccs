## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In accordance with **TASK-CCS-UX-002 Section 3**, the CraftEgon Corporate Services homepage was redesigned around the concrete operational needs of real customers.

Prior to redesign, the opening screen was overwhelmed by an internal 15-vertical taxonomy, complex entity intelligence lookups, and technical jargon that disoriented first-time founders.

The redesigned homepage immediately answers the four fundamental customer questions within the first viewport:
1. **What CCS Does:** Coordinated corporate secretarial, tax compliance, statutory licensing, and advisory services.
2. **Who CCS Serves:** Indian startups, growing SMEs, private limited companies, LLPs, and foreign subsidiaries.
3. **What Problems It Solves:** Eliminates incorporation delays, RoC late penalties, trademark vulnerabilities, and vendor fragmentation.
4. **What the Customer Should Do Next:** Browse featured services with upfront pricing indicators or submit an inquiry for a free scoping consultation.

## 3. Detailed Section Walkthrough

### Section 1: Hero & Primary CTA
- **Primary Display Headline:** `Your Business Infrastructure Partner.` (H1)
- **Clear Value Proposition:** Positions CCS as an infrastructure partner supporting companies from formation to compliance, protection, and growth.
- **Enterprise Lifecycle Badges:** 6 stages (`1. Establish`, `2. Operate`, `3. Comply`, `4. Protect`, `5. Grow`, `6. Continue`).
- **Universal Search Bar:** Real-time search indexing all 57 canonical services with keyboard navigation (`/`).
- **Direct Action CTAs:** High-visibility Primary button (*"Explore All 57 Services"*) and Secondary button (*"Talk to an Expert"*).
- **Verified Institutional Trust Row:** 3 core pillars (Qualified CS/CA Support, Pan-India MCA V3 Coverage, Transparent Execution).

### Section 2: Customer Problem & Solution Matrix
Addresses the four most acute compliance anxieties:
1. *Unpredictable Incorporation:* Replaced with pre-checked SPICe+ drafting and 4–7 business day CRC turnaround.
2. *Compounding Late Penalties:* Replaced with proactive secretarial calendar alerts before AOC-4/MGT-7 cutoffs.
3. *Brand & Trademark Vulnerability:* Replaced with 45-class phonetic clearances and Form TM-A filings.
4. *Fragmented Vendors:* Replaced with single-point corporate secretarial, tax, and licensing infrastructure.

### Section 3: Featured Launch Services Shelf (Core 5 Services)
Prominently features the five primary revenue drivers with indicative starting fees and realistic turnaround SLAs:
1. **Private Limited Registration (SPICe+):** Indicative Fee from ₹7,499 + Govt Fees | 4–7 Business Days.
2. **LLP Registration (FiLLiP):** Indicative Fee from ₹5,999 + Govt Fees | 5–8 Business Days.
3. **MSME / Udyam Registration:** Professional Assistance ₹999 (Zero Govt Fees) | 1–3 Working Days.
4. **Director DIR-3 KYC Verification:** Professional Fee ₹999 per DIN | Same-Day Filing.
5. **Trademark Registration (Form TM-A):** Indicative Fee from ₹4,499 + Govt Fees | 24–48 Hours to App No.

### Section 4: Key Services & Complete 15-Vertical Catalogue
Preserves the complete statutory taxonomy across 4 main functional categories and 15 practice verticals (`#practice-areas`), allowing sophisticated corporate clients and legal counsel to explore specialized areas (FEMA, M&A, DPDP Act, ESG).

### Section 5: Self-Service Diagnostic Tools & Estimators
Includes the RoC State Stamp Duty Calculator, STK-2 Strike-Off Eligibility Checker, and Trademark Classification screener.

### Section 6: How CCS Works (4-Step Engagement Lifecycle)
1. *Define Requirement:* Needs assessment and transparent fee roadmap.
2. *Document Verification:* Readiness checks preventing MCA resubmissions.
3. *Professional Execution:* Direct filing on MCA V3/GSTN by certified CS/CA professionals.
4. *Acknowledgement & Tracking:* Official government challans, SRN receipts, and annual compliance calendar inclusion.

### Section 7: Professional Standards & Regulatory Boundaries
Clear statutory disclosure explaining that non-contentious secretarial advisory and regulatory filings are handled directly, while contentious litigation and courtroom advocacy are strictly referred to independent bar members under the Advocates Act 1961.

### Section 8: Curated Educational Resources
Features the four most authoritative guides (SPICe+ Blueprint, RoC Penalties, MSME 43B(h), GST E-Invoicing) to demonstrate expertise without aggressive sales funnels.

### Section 9: Final Consultation Intake Desk
A clean 4-field inquiry form with clear expectation setting:
- Initial preliminary scoping is free of charge.
- Review by a qualified Company Secretary within 24 business hours (Mon–Sat, 9AM–7PM IST).
- Direct WhatsApp and Email links as instant alternative options.
