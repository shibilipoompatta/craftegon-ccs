Kerala Act 14 of 2025: Verified Gazette Chronology

The unified Kerala Societies Registration Act, 2025 (published Nov 7, 2025) officially commenced on January 1, 2026 via G.O.(P) No. 206/2025/TAXES (S.R.O. No. 1435/2025), repealing the split 1860 (Malabar) & 1955 (Travancore-Cochin) Acts. District Registrars (General) were formally appointed via S.R.O. No. 33/2026 (Jan 8, 2026).

Note on Subordinate Rules: Standardized digital portal fees and forms under the 2025 Act continue to roll out through Department of Registration notifications (registration.kerala.gov.in).

Mandatory Boundary: Registration ≠ 12AB / FCRA

Registering a society with the State Registrar does NOT grant Income Tax exemption or authorize foreign funding. You must apply separately for 12AB & 80G (Form 10A/10AB) for tax exemptions, NITI Aayog NGO Darpan for government grants, and MHA FCRA (SBI New Delhi Main Branch account) before accepting any foreign donations.

Frequently Asked Legal & Compliance Questions

What happened to existing Kerala societies registered under the 1955 Travancore-Cochin Act or 1860 Act?

Under the transitional savings provisions of the Kerala Societies Registration Act, 2025 (Act 14 of 2025)—which commenced on January 1, 2026—all compliant societies validly registered under the earlier Travancore-Cochin Act, 1955 or the central 1860 Act (Malabar region) are deemed registered under the new Act. Going forward, their annual AGM filings (within 21 days under Sec 19) and audits (Sec 18) are governed by the 2025 Act.

Should I register a Society or a Section 8 Company for my NGO?

A Registered Society is ideal for community welfare groups, clubs, resident associations, and regional cultural/educational bodies with 7+ members seeking lower compliance cost under state district registrars. However, national foundations, CSR implementation agencies, and high-value grant-making entities often prefer a Section 8 Non-Profit Company because it needs only 2 directors, carries corporate credibility under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), and is governed uniformly nationwide.

Can family members form a society together?

While the parent Acts do not universally bar relatives from joining the General Body, several State Registrars require administrative affidavits confirming that Governing Body office bearers (e.g., President, Secretary, Treasurer) are not closely related by blood or marriage to ensure institutional transparency and arm's-length public governance.
