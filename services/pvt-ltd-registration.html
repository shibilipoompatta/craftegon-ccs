Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

A Private Limited Company is India's most recognized corporate structure for venture-backed startups, technology businesses, manufacturing enterprises, and foreign subsidiary establishments seeking limited liability and separate legal personality.

Mandatory Statutory Requirements under Companies Act, 2013:

  • Minimum Directors: At least 2 directors (maximum 15). At least one director must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for ≥ 182 days during the financial year under Section 149(3).
  • Minimum Shareholders: At least 2 shareholders (individuals or corporate bodies, up to a maximum of 200 members under Section 2(68)). Directors can also be shareholders.
  • Minimum Capital: No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement under the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015. Authorized capital can start at ₹1,00,000.
  • Registered Office: A physical commercial or residential address located in India where statutory communications can be received.

Scope of Work & Exclusions

Included in Scope

Statutory Inclusions:

  • Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) with encryption for 2 directors.
  • Director Identification Number (DIN) allocation for up to 3 directors via SPICe+.
  • Name availability check & reservation via SPICe+ Part A (RUN protocol).
  • Statutory drafting of electronic Memorandum of Association (e-MOA, Form INC-33) and electronic Articles of Association (e-AOA, Form INC-34).
  • SPICe+ Part B incorporation filing with MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC).
  • Integrated AGILE-PRO-S filing: mandatory EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax (select states), and Bank Current Account opening integration.
  • Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) issuance.
  • Certificate of Incorporation (COI) issued by Registrar of Companies with Corporate Identity Number (CIN).
Post-Incorporation Compliance (Separate)

Exclusions & Subsequent Obligations:

  • Form INC-20A: Mandatory Declaration of Commencement of Business filed within 180 days after capital subscription deposit (available under separate secretarial mandate).
  • Form ADT-1: Statutory Auditor Appointment within 30 days of incorporation by the Board of Directors.
  • State Stamp Duty on Authorized Capital: Stamp duties vary by state jurisdiction (e.g., Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, Kerala) and are charged at actuals.
  • Annual RoC Compliance: First financial year balance sheet (AOC-4) and annual return (MGT-7/7A) filings.
  • Trademark / IP Registration: Form TM-A brand protection must be filed separately under IPR practice.

Required Documents & Information Checklist

1. Director & Shareholder KYC

  • Self-attested PAN Card (mandatory for Indian nationals).
  • Passport (mandatory for foreign nationals, apostilled/consularized if overseas).
  • Identity Proof: Voter ID / Passport / Driving License.
  • Address Proof: Bank Statement / Electricity Bill / Telephone Bill (not older than 2 months).
  • Passport-size digital photograph.

2. Registered Office Proof

  • Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Gas Bill of the registered premises (not older than 2 months).
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) from the legal property owner.
  • Notarized Rent/Lease Agreement (if rented premises).
  • Proof of ownership (Tax receipt / Title deed) if owned.

3. Corporate & Entity Structuring Data

  • Proposed company name options (in order of preference).
  • Main object clause describing intended business activities.
  • Proposed Authorized Capital and Paid-up Capital share ratio.
  • Nominated bank for integrated current account opening.

Statutory Process & Realistic Timelines

1
Digital Signature & Document Compilation (Day 1–2)

Verification of KYC documents and issuance of Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) with hardware tokens for all proposed directors.

2
Name Availability Reservation via SPICe+ Part A (Day 2–3)

Filing of name reservation with MCA CRC. Names must adhere strictly to Rule 8 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014 and Trademark Registry clearance.

3
Charter Drafting & SPICe+ Part B E-Filing (Day 3–5)

Drafting customized MoA & AoA clauses, affixing director and professional DSCs (CS/CA), compiling declarations (INC-9, DIR-2), and uploading forms to MCA V3 portal.

4
CRC Approval & Certificate of Incorporation Issuance (Day 5–7)

Upon scrutiny and approval by the Registrar of Companies (CRC), the Certificate of Incorporation is generated containing the CIN, PAN, and TAN.
