01 / Service overview

One compliance desk. Two distinct legal frameworks.

Companies and LLPs have different statutes, forms, approval processes and filing timelines. CCS scopes each entity separately instead of treating the two as interchangeable. The exact annual obligations depend on the entity's classification, financials, capital, transactions, ownership and event history.

COMPANIES ACT, 2013

Private Limited Companies

Annual financial statement and annual return filings, AGM and Board documentation, statutory record coordination and conditional MCA filings.

  • AOC-4 family, as applicable
  • MGT-7 or MGT-7A, based on eligibility
  • AGM, Board and statutory register support
  • Conditional and event-based form tracking
LLP ACT, 2008

Limited Liability Partnerships

Annual statement of account and solvency, annual return, partner and designated partner records, agreement updates and audit coordination.

  • LLP Form 8
  • LLP Form 11
  • Form 3 / Form 4 when changes occur
  • Audit applicability and annual record review

03 / Key legal updates

Regulatory changes businesses should not miss

Director KYC: three-year cycle from 31 March 2026

The MCA's G.S.R. 943(E), dated 31 December 2025, replaces the annual KYC cycle with a KYC intimation once every third consecutive financial year, generally due by 30 June following that cycle. Changes to personal mobile number, email or residential address must be reported within 30 days. ICAI's 2 January 2026 announcement explains the transition; directors with KYC completed under the earlier regime were stated to have their next KYC due by 30 June 2028.

Small company thresholds revised from 1 December 2025

G.S.R. 880(E) sets the prescribed small-company thresholds at paid-up capital not exceeding ₹10 crore and turnover not exceeding ₹100 crore. Section 2(85) requires both prescribed tests to be met: paid-up capital must not exceed ₹10 crore AND preceding-year turnover must not exceed ₹100 crore. The statutory exclusions—including holding companies, subsidiary companies, section 8 companies and entities governed by a special Act—also apply. Classification should be tested for the relevant financial year and not assumed from turnover alone.

LLP audit threshold and Form 8 certification

LLP Rules, Rule 24 contains the audit exemption framework. MCA's Form 8 instruction kit states that auditor certification is required where LLP turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or partner contribution obligation exceeds ₹25 lakh. Confirm current facts and rule wording with the appointed professional before relying on the exemption.

LLP financial statements: ICAI guidance

ICAI announced on 31 March 2026 a phased applicability of its Guidance Note on Financial Statements of LLPs: Phase I for periods beginning on/after 1 April 2025 for entities with turnover above ₹5 crore; Phase II for periods beginning on/after 1 April 2026 for all entities. Financial reporting teams should consider the applicable phase.

07 / Frequently asked questions

Questions founders and directors ask

Does a company have to file annual returns if it had no business?

Generally yes. A company remains subject to applicable annual filing and governance requirements even if it has nil turnover or no active business, unless a specific statutory exemption or status changes the obligation. Dormant status is a separate statutory process; inactivity alone does not remove filings.

Does every company file MGT-7?

No. Eligible small companies and One Person Companies generally use MGT-7A. Other companies use MGT-7. Confirm small-company eligibility under section 2(85), including statutory exclusions and current prescribed thresholds.

Is DIR-3 KYC still an annual filing?

No. From 31 March 2026, the amended Rule 12A provides for KYC intimation once every third consecutive financial year, by 30 June following that cycle. Changes to mobile number, email or residential address must be reported within 30 days.

Is ADT-1 filed every year?

Not automatically. ADT-1 is associated with the appointment of an auditor and the applicable filing requirement. A routine AGM does not by itself mean a fresh ADT-1 is required every year during an existing valid appointment.

Are Form 8 and Form 11 required for every LLP?

Every registered LLP is generally required to file its annual return in Form 11 and statement of account and solvency in Form 8, even where it has little or no business. The content, certification and audit requirements depend on the LLP's facts and current rules.

Does the annual retainer include statutory audit and government fees?

Not unless expressly stated in the signed engagement. Independent audit and professional certification are separately performed by eligible professionals. MCA fees, additional fees, stamp duty, taxes and third-party costs should be identified separately in the written quote.

What if the company or LLP has old filing defaults?

A fresh annual retainer does not automatically regularise historic defaults. CCS should first conduct a filing-status review, identify pending forms and exposure, and provide a separate remediation scope with professional review where required.
