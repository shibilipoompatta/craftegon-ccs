Director Disqualification under Section 164(2) & DIN Restoration

Legal implications of corporate defaults on director identification numbers (DIN), statutory debarment periods, and high court writ or NCLT remediation pathways.

1. Grounds for Disqualification under Section 164(2)

A person cannot be re-appointed as a director of another company or hold office in a defaulting company for 5 years if the company in which they are a director fails to:

  • File financial statements or annual returns for any continuous period of 3 financial years.
  • Repay deposits accepted, pay interest thereon, redeem any debentures, or pay declared dividends for one year or more.

2. DIN Deactivation & Legal Remedies

When disqualification triggers, the MCA automatically deactivates the Director Identification Number (DIN), preventing digital signature signings or new company formations. Restoration requires:

  1. Filing Writ Petitions before the jurisdictional High Court challenging arbitrary DIN deactivation without due notice, or
  2. Utilizing Condonation of Delay Schemes (CODS) when promulgated by the Central Government to clear pending defaults and restore DIN status.
