Close down dormant entities lawfully or manage corporate restructuring and distressed asset resolutions through fast-track company strike-offs (Form STK-2), voluntary liquidations, and Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) frameworks.

Core Exit & Insolvency Services

Fast-Track Company Strike-Off (Form STK-2)

Lawful closure and removal of name for defunct or inoperative companies under Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013.

LLP Strike-Off (Form 24)

Simplified statutory closure process for defunct Limited Liability Partnerships with zero commercial liabilities.

Dormant Status Application (Form MSC-1)

Obtaining dormant company status under Section 455 to preserve intellectual property or real estate while minimizing compliance overhead.

Corporate Insolvency & CIRP Defense

Creditor and debtor representation under corporate insolvency resolution processes, settlement negotiations, and Section 12A withdrawals.
