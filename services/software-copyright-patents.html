Frequently Asked Questions

Is software patentable in India?
Under Section 3(k) of the Patents Act, a computer program per se is not patentable. However, if the software produces a technical effect or is tied to novel hardware demonstrating technical advancement, it qualifies as a Computer-Related Invention (CRI).
How long does software copyright protection last in India?
Software copyright lasts for 60 years from the year following the publication date (or 60 years from the author's death for individual authors).
