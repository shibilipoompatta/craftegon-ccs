Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What are the eligibility conditions for a Fast-Track Merger under Section 233 of Companies Act 2013? +
Section 233 fast-track mergers bypass NCLT court hearings and are processed via the Regional Director (RD) and Official Liquidator (OL) exclusively for mergers between two or more Small Companies, or between a Holding Company and its Wholly-Owned Subsidiary (WOS).
What is the tax implication of a Slump Sale via Business Transfer Agreement (BTA)? +
Under Section 50B of the Income Tax Act 1961, transfer of an undertaking on a going-concern basis for a lump-sum consideration without assigning individual values to assets attracts capital gains tax based on the net worth of the undertaking.
