Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

An LLP is the preferred statutory structure for service businesses, professional advisors (engineers, architects, consultants, creative agencies), and joint ventures that desire limited liability without the rigid secretarial burden, mandatory statutory audits, and board meeting rituals of a Private Limited Company.

Mandatory Statutory Eligibility Requirements under the LLP Act, 2008 & 2021 Amendment:

  • Minimum Partners: At least 2 partners (individuals or corporate bodies). No upper limit on the maximum number of partners under Section 5.
  • Designated Partners (DPIN): At least 2 individual partners must act as "Designated Partners", responsible for statutory filings and legal compliance under Section 7.
  • Indian Resident Partner (Amended Section 7(1)): Under Section 7(1) proviso as amended by the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021 (effective April 1, 2022), at least one Designated Partner must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for not less than 120 days during the financial year (relaxed from the previous 182-day rule).
  • Capital Contribution: No statutory minimum capital threshold. Partners can contribute tangible or intangible property, cash, or agreements to contribute.
  • Registered Office: A physical premises address within India where statutory communications can be received.

Statutory DPIN Ceiling in Form FiLLiP: Maximum 2 Applicants

Under MCA V3 portal architecture, Form FiLLiP allows DPIN/DIN allocation for a maximum of 2 individuals who do not already possess a DIN/DPIN. If your proposed LLP will have 3 or more Designated Partners who require fresh identification numbers, the 3rd and subsequent individual partners must apply in advance for a Director Identification Number (DIN) using Form DIR-3 under the Companies Act, 2013. The LLP Agreement & Form 3: Critical Contractual & Tax Rules

Special Legal Notice: Under Section 23 of the LLP Act, the mutual rights and duties of partners are governed strictly by the written LLP Agreement. Form 3 is not the agreement; Form 3 is the mandatory statutory e-form used to file the details of the already-executed and stamped agreement with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days of incorporation.

The First Schedule Default Trap & Income Tax Section 40(b) Disallowance

If partners fail to execute a customized LLP agreement, Section 23(4) enforces the default provisions of the First Schedule to the LLP Act, 2008. Under Clause 2 of the First Schedule, no partner is entitled to any remuneration. Under Section 40(b)(v) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, partner remuneration is only deductible if expressly authorized by the registered deed. Relying on default rules results in 100% disallowance of partner salaries, triggering punitive tax at 30% + surcharge. Furthermore, default rules mandate equal 50:50 profit sharing regardless of capital and bar partner expulsion under Section 24(5). Frequently Asked Questions (LLP FAQs)

What is the Indian residency requirement for Designated Partners in an LLP?

Under Section 7(1) proviso as amended by the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021 (effective April 1, 2022), at least one Designated Partner must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for not less than 120 days during the financial year. The historical 182-day requirement was officially relaxed to harmonize LLP rules with corporate governance standards.

Is statutory audit mandatory for all LLPs in India?

No. Under Section 34(4) of the LLP Act and Rule 24 of LLP Rules, an LLP is exempt from mandatory statutory audit if its annual turnover does not exceed ₹40 Lakhs and its total capital contribution does not exceed ₹25 Lakhs. Once either threshold is crossed in a financial year, a Chartered Accountant audit is mandatory.

What are the statutory late fees if Form 3 is not filed within 30 days?

Under substituted Section 69 of the LLP Act (effective April 1, 2022) and Annexure A of the LLP Rules, the previous flat penalty of ₹100 per day without ceiling has been replaced by graduated fee multiples (1x to 25x of the normal statutory filing fee) based on the period of delay. Furthermore, under Section 76A, Small LLPs enjoy a 50% reduction on these statutory delay fees.

Can more than 2 Designated Partners get DPINs directly through FiLLiP?

No. Form FiLLiP only accommodates up to 2 individuals without an existing DIN/DPIN. If your LLP requires 3 or more Designated Partners who do not yet have a DIN, the 3rd and subsequent individuals must apply separately through Form DIR-3 prior to filing FiLLiP.

Why is relying on the default First Schedule dangerous for partner salaries?

Under Clause 2 of the First Schedule to the LLP Act, partners are not entitled to remuneration. Under Section 40(b) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, remuneration paid to working partners cannot be deducted from business profits unless expressly provided in the registered LLP agreement. Without an agreement clause, all salaries are disallowed, subjecting the firm to 30% tax + surcharge.
