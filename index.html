Statutory FAQs

High-Intent Regulatory Q&A

Authoritative legal clarifications grounded in Indian corporate and cross-border statutes.

What are the mandatory annual RoC filing forms for an Indian Private Limited Company? +
Under the Companies Act, 2013, every private limited company must file Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements within 30 days of AGM under Section 137), Form MGT-7/MGT-7A (Annual Return within 60 days of AGM under Section 92), Form DIR-3 KYC for all active directors, Form DPT-3 (Return of Deposits/Outstanding Loans by June 30th), and Form MSME-1 half-yearly.
What is the penalty for late filing of Form AOC-4 and MGT-7? +
Under Section 403 read with the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, delay attracts an additional fee of ₹100 per day per form with no statutory ceiling, alongside substantive monetary penalties on the company and defaulting directors under Section 137(3) and Section 92(5).
Who is required to file the annual RBI Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) Return? +
Any Indian company, LLP, or alternative investment fund that has received Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) or made Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) in any previous financial year must submit the FLA return online via the RBI FLAIR portal on or before July 15th annually under FEMA regulations.
