Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

Is DGFT Import Export Code (IEC) required to be updated annually? +
Yes, as per DGFT Public Notice No. 58/2015-2020, an IEC holder must electronically confirm and update its IEC details between April and June every year on the DGFT portal, even if there are no changes, failing which the IEC is deactivated.
When is a Central FSSAI License mandatory over a State License? +
A Central FSSAI License is required for food business operators (FBOs) operating in multiple states with a head office, 100% export-oriented units (EOUs), large manufacturers exceeding 2 Metric Tonnes/day capacity, or businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₹20 Crores.
