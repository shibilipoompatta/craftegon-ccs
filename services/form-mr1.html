Form MR-1 MD/WTD Appointment Return

Mandatory return filed with the ROC certifying the appointment, tenure, and executive remuneration terms of Managing Directors, Whole-Time Directors, or Managers.

Statutory Authority

Section 196 & Section 197 read with Schedule V of Companies Act, 2013 & Rule 7 of Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014

Governing Jurisdiction

Republic of India (Central Portal)

Execution SLA

3 to 5 Business Days

Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory return filed with the ROC certifying the appointment, tenure, and executive remuneration terms of Managing Directors, Whole-Time Directors, or Managers.

Statutory Mandate: Section 196 & Section 197 read with Schedule V of Companies Act, 2013 & Rule 7 of Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Unlisted public companies appointing executive managerial personnel under Section 196 (Private limited companies are exempt per Notification G.S.R. 464(E)).

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Board resolution and general meeting special/ordinary resolution approving appointment
  • Written employment agreement / appointment terms outlining executive remuneration and perks
  • Form DIR-2 (Consent to act as Director) and Form DIR-8 (Intimation of non-disqualification)
  • Statutory declaration confirming compliance with Schedule V Part I eligibility criteria

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Verify appointee eligibility under Schedule V (age 21–70, non-conviction under specified statutes)
  2. Step 2: Obtain Board and General Meeting approvals with detailed explanatory statements
  3. Step 3: Access Form MR-1 on MCA V3 web portal within 60 days of appointment date
  4. Step 4: Input DIN, tenure dates, commission, salary, and attach employment contract
  5. Step 5: Affix Director DSC and certifying PCS DSC; submit and pay fee via Bharatkosh

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Form MR-1 apply to Private Limited Companies?
No. Private limited companies are fully exempt from filing Form MR-1 pursuant to MCA Exemption Notification G.S.R. 464(E).
Can a person aged above 70 be appointed as Managing Director?
Yes, provided a Special Resolution is passed by shareholders with an explanatory statement justifying the appointment.
