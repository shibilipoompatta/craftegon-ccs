Professional Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2026

1. No Unsolicited Legal or Formal Retainer

The content, guides, tools, and calculators provided on the CraftEgon CCS platform are for informational and educational purposes only and do not constitute formal legal, tax, or accounting advice. Accessing our web portals or submitting inquiry forms does not establish a professional advocate-client or chartered accountant-client relationship until a formal engagement agreement is executed.

2. Regulatory Changes

While every effort is made to ensure accuracy regarding MCA V3 portals, Income Tax, GST, and FEMA regulations, statutory rules are subject to continuous legislative amendments. Clients are advised to seek tailored professional counsel for specific transactions.

3. Corporate Secretarial Scope & Independent Legal Counsel

CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial, statutory compliance, and document drafting infrastructure only. CraftEgon CCS does not enter appearances, handle contentious litigation, or provide formal tribunal advocacy before courts or tribunals. Clients requiring formal legal representation must engage an independent advocate enrolled under the Advocates Act, 1961.
