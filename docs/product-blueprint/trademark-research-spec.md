## 1. Product Overview & Ethical Boundaries

The **CraftEgon Trademark & IPR Research Suite** provides brand founders and legal counsel with preliminary trademark similarity screening, Nice Classification guidance (Classes 1–45), and application milestone tracking.

### Strict Ethical Guardrails
1. **Never Guarantee Registration**: A clean preliminary search does NOT guarantee trademark registration. Only the Trade Marks Registry (TMR) Examiner has the statutory authority to approve or object to Form TM-A under Section 9 (Absolute Grounds) and Section 11 (Relative Grounds).
2. **Clear Source Disclaimers**: Display prominent notices stating that preliminary results reflect public registry data and require a formal trademark attorney search opinion before filing.

## 3. Nice Classification (Class 1–45) Architecture

| Core Sector | Primary Nice Classes | Cross-Search Recommendations | Typical CraftEgon Client Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SaaS & Software** | **Class 9** (Software, downloadable apps)<br>**Class 42** (Cloud platforms, SaaS, IT services) | Class 35 (E-commerce), Class 38 (Telecom/Chat) | Tech startups, B2B SaaS, Mobile App studios |
| **Corporate Advisory** | **Class 35** (Business management, consulting)<br>**Class 36** (Financial, accounting services)<br>**Class 45** (Legal services, regulatory compliance) | Class 41 (Training/Seminars) | Consulting firms, FinTechs, Accounting desks |
| **E-Commerce & Retail** | **Class 35** (Online retail, marketplace services) | Relevant Product Classes (Classes 3, 25, 29, 30) | D2C brands, Marketplace aggregators |
| **Healthcare & Pharma** | **Class 5** (Pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals)<br>**Class 44** (Medical, diagnostic services) | Class 10 (Medical devices), Class 35 | HealthTech, Diagnostic labs, BioTech ventures |
