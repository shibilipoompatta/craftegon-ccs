Corporate Compliance & RoC Secretarial

Protect your entity from additional MCA fees, director disqualification, and strike-off notices. Supervised by qualified Company Secretaries under the Companies Act 2013.

Corporate Secretarial & RoC Filings

Annual Secretarial Retainer

Complete annual compliance coverage including statutory registers, board meeting minutes, AGM drafting, AOC-4, and MGT-7 filings.

View Retainer Scope →

Form AOC-4 Financial Statements

Mandatory annual filing of audited balance sheet, profit and loss account, directors' report, and auditor's report with the RoC.

View Form AOC-4 Scope →

Form MGT-7/7A Annual Return

Annual statutory return detailing shareholding structure, directorship changes, board meetings, and member registers under Section 92.

View Form MGT-7 Scope →

DIR-3 KYC Director Verification

Statutory director verification and personal contact updates to keep DIN credentials active and compliant on MCA V3.

View DIR-3 KYC Scope →

Fast-Track Strike Off (STK-2)

Legal dissolution for defunct and inoperative companies under Section 248. Complete closing statement and RoC closure order.

View Strike Off Scope →

Dormant Company Status (MSC-1)

Application under Section 455 to obtain dormant status for holding IP or future projects with minimal annual compliance maintenance.
