Who This Service Is For & Statutory Deadlines

Every active Private Limited Company, One Person Company (OPC), Section 8 Company, and LLP incorporated in India is legally mandated to file annual financial statements and annual returns with the Registrar of Companies (RoC), irrespective of turnover or operational activity.

Statutory Annual Milestone Calendar:

  • Annual General Meeting (AGM): Must be convened within 6 months of the close of the financial year (on or before September 30 under Section 96). First AGM can be held within 9 months of first FY close.
  • DIR-3 KYC: Mandatory annual verification for all DIN holders on or before September 30. DINs are deactivated with ₹5,000 reactivation fee if missed.
  • Form AOC-4 / AOC-4 XBRL: Filing of audited financial statements within 30 days of AGM (typically on or before October 30 under Section 137).
  • Form MGT-7 / MGT-7A: Filing of annual return within 60 days of AGM (typically on or before November 29 under Section 92). Small companies file abbreviated Form MGT-7A.

Exclusions & Separate Filings

Exclusions & Event-Based Requirements:

  • Independent Statutory Audit: The audit report must be prepared and signed by your independent statutory auditor / Chartered Accountant firm with valid UDIN.
  • Event-Based Filings: Director appointments/resignations (DIR-12), registered office shifts (INC-22), or share capital increases (SH-7) are billed per event.
  • Form DPT-3: Return of deposits and outstanding loans (due June 30, available under Enterprise plan).
  • Compounding Petitions: Section 441 compounding for legacy defaults before Regional Director or NCLT requires separate mandate with independent legal counsel.
  • Government Late Fees: MCA normal and additional penalty fees are paid at actuals.

Required Documents & Financial Information

1. Audited Financial Statements

  • Audited Balance Sheet and Statement of Profit & Loss.
  • Notes to Accounts and Significant Accounting Policies.
  • Cash Flow Statement (if applicable / non-small company).
  • Statutory Auditor's Report with ICAI UDIN.

2. Governance & Corporate Records

  • Draft Directors' Report signed by 2 directors (including Chairman if designated).
  • List of share transfers, transmissions, or allotments during the financial year.
  • Details of Board Meetings held during the year (minimum 4 meetings with gap ≤ 120 days).
  • Details of loans taken from directors or banking institutions.

3. Digital Credentials & Signatures

  • Active Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) of at least 1 authorized director.
  • MCA V3 portal user login credentials.
  • Practicing professional DSC (PCS/PCA) for annual return certification (if applicable).

Statutory Process & Late Fee Risk Mitigation

1
Audit Review & Board Meeting Convening (August–September)

Receipt of signed audited accounts. Board meeting convened with 7 days' statutory notice to adopt financials, approve the Director's Report, and issue the AGM notice.

2
DIR-3 KYC Verification Completed (Deadline: September 30)

Annual director verification submitted online. Prevents DIN deactivation and ₹5,000 statutory penalty per defaulting director.

3
Form AOC-4 Financial Statements Filing (Within 30 Days of AGM — October 30)

Compilation, pre-scrutiny, and electronic submission of financial statements with the RoC. Eliminates Section 403 cumulative ₹100/day penalty exposure.

4
Form MGT-7/7A Annual Return Filing (Within 60 Days of AGM — November 29)

Filing of comprehensive annual return. Challan download, SRN archiving, and statutory registers update.
