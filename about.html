Regulatory Boundaries

Compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961

Clear Demarcation: Secretarial Infrastructure vs. Courtroom Advocacy

In strict adherence to the Advocates Act, 1961 and the regulatory standards prescribed by the Bar Council of India, CraftEgon Corporate Services does not provide courtroom appearances, tribunal pleadings, or contentious litigation advocacy.

  • What CCS Delivers: Non-contentious corporate secretarial documentation, RoC and MCA e-filings, statutory register maintenance, tax compliance procedural assistance, and pre-litigation documentation audit.
  • Contentious Litigation Referral: When a matter escalates to formal tribunal dispute (NCLT, NCLAT, High Court, or Income Tax Appellate Tribunal), formal advocacy must be conducted by independent practicing advocates engaged on a separate vakalatnama.
