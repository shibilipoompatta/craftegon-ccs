Statutory Pathways

Every Business Problem Has a Statutory Solution

Real corporate compliance challenges Indian founders face — and the exact regulatory pathways to resolve each one without penalties or legal exposure.

My company missed ROC annual filing deadlines (AOC-4 / MGT-7)

Overdue financial statements and annual returns trigger cumulative Section 403 additional fees (₹100/day). We perform immediate statutory reconciliations, regularize delinquent filings under MCA V3, and coordinate Regional Director compounding under Section 441 where necessary.

Regularize RoC Filings →

I need a company name reserved quickly before competitors register it

Fast-track name reservation through MCA SPICe+ Part A (RUN-N) with preliminary Trademark Class search. Our compliance desk conducts cross-class conflict checks and submits within 24 hours of onboarding.

Reserve Company Name →

A director's DIN is marked 'DEACTIVATED' or disqualified on MCA V3

DIN deactivation due to missed annual DIR-3 KYC or non-filing of financial statements under Section 164(2). We file retrospective DIR-3 KYC e-forms and draft statutory revival petitions to restore active directorial standing.

Reactivate Director DIN →

I want to transition my Sole Proprietorship into a Private Limited Company

Seamless conversion executed via slump sale agreement, business takeover pact, and fresh SPICe+ company incorporation. We preserve existing customer goodwill, transfer operational contracts, and migrate GSTIN and MSME Udyam credentials cleanly.

Structure Entity Conversion →

Our startup received foreign investment without filing Form FC-GPR

Under RBI FEMA Regulation 20(R), inbound FDI requires reporting on the FIRMS Single Master Form within 30 days. We compile FIRC and KYC audit trails from Authorised Dealer banks and file compounding dossiers under the Late Submission Fee (LSF) window.

Resolve FEMA Inbound Filings →

We crossed threshold headcount and need mandatory EPFO & ESIC codes

Mandatory establishment registration under the Employees' Provident Funds Act (1952) and ESI Act (1948). We secure statutory registration codes, establish monthly contribution systems, and ensure full labour code compliance.
