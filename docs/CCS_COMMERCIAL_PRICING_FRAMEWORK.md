## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Governance

This framework establishes the financial, cost-accounting, and commercial pricing model for CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS). It operationalizes the transition from unverified theoretical rates into legally sound, economically sustainable, and audit-compliant commercial pricing.

### Mandatory Commercial Governance Principles:
1. **Zero Unapproved Publication:** No fixed commercial price may be published on public web pages or presented as an approved commercial quote until the business owner executes written partner fee agreements and approves commercial schedules.
2. **Pure Agent Statutory Disbursement Treatment:** Government fees, stamp duties, and portal challans must be treated strictly as **Pure Agent Disbursements** under Rule 33 of the CGST Rules, 2017. CCS collects these amounts at actual cost without markup or GST imposition.
3. **Separation of Fees and Liabilities:** Every client invoice must cleanly distinguish CCS Service Coordination Fees from Partner Professional Execution Fees and Government Disbursements.

## 4. Payment Terms, Milestones & Financial Policies

### A. Advance Payments & Disbursement Policy
1. **100% Advance on Statutory Disbursements:** Government fees, MCA challans, stamp duties, and trademark official fees must be collected in full before filing. Under no circumstances may CCS fund government disbursements out of operating capital.
2. **50% Advance on Professional Fees:** Engagements commence only upon receipt of 50% advance coordination and partner fees.
3. **Milestone Payments (for Formation Services):**
   - Milestone 1 (50% Professional + 100% Disbursements): Upon Name Reservation approval.
   - Milestone 2 (50% Professional balance): Upon generation of Certificate of Incorporation (CoI) or official submission receipt.

### B. Cancellation & Refund Policy
1. **Statutory Disbursements:** Strictly non-refundable once challan is generated on the MCA, GSTN, or IP India portal.
2. **Coordination Fees:**
   - Cancellation prior to document drafting: 80% refund (20% administrative retention).
   - Cancellation after drafting but prior to portal submission: 40% refund.
   - Cancellation after portal submission: Zero refund.

### C. Additional Work & Resubmission Surcharges
1. **MCA Resubmissions:** MCA V3 provides up to two resubmissions for technical queries. Resubmissions arising from client data errors (e.g. incorrect PAN, name mismatch) are billed at ₹1,000 + GST per resubmission.
2. **Defective / Expired DSCs:** Re-issuance of client Digital Signature Certificates due to lost tokens or forgotten PINs is billed at actual token costs (₹1,000 + GST).
