- **Vertical Hub**: [`verticals/01-entity-formation.html`](file:///c:/Users/shibi/OneDrive/Desktop/craftegon-ccs/verticals/01-entity-formation.html)
- **Key Services**:
  1. *Private Limited Company (SPICe+ Part A & B)* (`services/pvt-ltd-registration.html`)
  2. *Limited Liability Partnership (FiLLiP)* (`services/llp-incorporation.html`)
  3. *One Person Company (OPC)* (`services/opc-registration.html`)
  4. *Section 8 Non-Profit / NGO* (`services/section-8-registration.html`)
  5. *DPIIT Startup India Recognition* (`services/dpiit-startup-india.html`)
- **Customer Problem**: Navigating MCA V3 incorporation hurdles, name rejections, and DSC token provisioning.
- **Statutory Eligibility**: Min 2 directors (1 Indian resident) and 2 shareholders for Pvt Ltd; min 2 designated partners for LLP.
- **Scope of Work**: Name approval (RUN/SPICe+ Part A), MoA/AoA drafting, DIN allotment, PAN/TAN issuance, EPF/ESIC/Professional Tax registration, bank account linkage.
- **Exclusions**: Payment of state stamp duties above standard nominal capital slabs; non-standard MoA objects requiring sectoral approvals (e.g. RBI NBFC, SEBI, IRDAI).
- **Client Inputs Required**: PAN card, Aadhaar / Passport of directors, bank statements/electricity bills (utility proof < 2 months old), registered office NOC, specimen signature.
- **Deliverables**: Certificate of Incorporation (CoI), e-PAN, e-TAN, signed MoA & AoA, DIN allocation letters, digital DSC tokens.
- **Regulatory Basis**: Companies Act 2013 (Section 3, 7, 12); Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014; LLP Act, 2008.
- **Indicative Professional Fee**: ₹7,499 (Pvt Ltd base) / ₹5,999 (LLP base) + statutory state stamp duty and MCA challans.
- **Timeline**: 3–7 business days (subject to MCA Central Registration Centre processing).
- **Escalation Desk**: WhatsApp `+91 86069 18247` &bull; Email `admin@craftegon.com`.

### V02 — RoC Compliance & Secretarial Retainers
- **Vertical Hub**: [`verticals/02-roc-compliance.html`](file:///c:/Users/shibi/OneDrive/Desktop/craftegon-ccs/verticals/02-roc-compliance.html)
- **Key Services**:
  1. *RoC Master Annual Retainer* (`services/roc-annual-compliance.html`)
  2. *Form AOC-4 Financial Statements Filing* (`services/aoc4-xbrl.html`)
  3. *Form MGT-7 / MGT-7A Annual Return* (`services/mgt7-filing.html`)
  4. *DIR-3 KYC Annual Director Verification* (`services/dir3-kyc.html`)
  5. *Form DPT-3 Return of Deposits* (`services/dpt3-filing.html`)
  6. *Form MSME-1 Half-Yearly Return* (`services/msme1-filing.html`)
  7. *Form INC-20A Commencement of Business* (`services/inc20a-commencement.html`)
  8. *Statutory Register Maintenance* (`services/board-minutes.html`)
- **Customer Problem**: Accumulating mandatory late fees under Section 403 (₹100/day per form with no upper cap) and risk of director disqualification under Section 164(2).
- **Statutory Deadlines**:
  - `Form AOC-4`: Within 30 days of Annual General Meeting (typically October 30).
  - `Form MGT-7`: Within 60 days of Annual General Meeting (typically November 29).
  - `DIR-3 KYC`: Annually on or before September 30 (flat ₹5,000 penalty post-deadline).
  - `Form DPT-3`: Annually on or before June 30.
  - `Form MSME-1`: Half-yearly (April 30 and October 31).
  - `Form INC-20A`: Within 180 days of incorporation date.
- **Scope of Work**: Drafting notice, agenda & minutes of Board Meetings and AGM; preparing Directors' Report; XBRL tagging of balance sheet/P&L; filing RoC forms with CS certification.
- **Regulatory Basis**: Companies Act 2013 (Sections 92, 137, 134, 73, 196); MCA Fee Rules 2014.
- **Indicative Professional Fee**: ₹14,999/year (Standard Pvt Ltd retainer) + MCA challans.
