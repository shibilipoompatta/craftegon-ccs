## 1. Executive Summary & Measurement Integrity Governance

This specification establishes the management metrics, formulas, data sources, and operational dashboard architecture for CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS). It operationalizes business health tracking, cost controls, quality assurance, and founder independence.

### Measurement Integrity Mandate:
In strict accordance with Task Section 8:
1. **Zero Invented Baselines:** No speculative historical numbers or premature performance targets are invented. All performance benchmarks must be established through empirical observation during a controlled **90-Day Operational Baseline Period**.
2. **Formula Transparency:** Every operational measure is defined with an unambiguous mathematical formula, explicit data inputs, and direct attribution to one of the five operational registers (`LEAD-REG`, `CLIENT-REG`, `MATTER-REG`, `PARTNER-REG`, `BILLING-REG`).

## 2. Core Operational Metrics & Calculation Methods

### A. Acquisition & Conversion Metrics

#### 1. Qualified Enquiries (QE)
- **Definition:** Total number of unique corporate service inquiries received during the period that meet minimum jurisdictional, entity, and statutory scope criteria.
- **Formula:** $\text{QE} = \sum \text{Inquiries in } \texttt{LEAD-REG} \text{ with } \text{status} \neq \text{\"SPAM\"} \land \text{status} \neq \text{\"OUT_OF_SCOPE\"}$
- **Source:** `LEAD-REG` | **Frequency:** Weekly / Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED (Day 1 to 90)`

#### 2. Conversion Rate (CR)
- **Definition:** Percentage of qualified enquiries that convert into executed, paid client engagements.
- **Formula:** $\text{CR} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Engagements Executed with Advance Received}}{\text{Total Qualified Enquiries}} \right) \times 100$
- **Source:** `LEAD-REG` cross-referenced with `BILLING-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED (Day 1 to 90)`

### B. Financial & Revenue Metrics

#### 3. Revenue Collected (Gross Collections)
- **Definition:** Total gross funds collected from clients, encompassing service fees, professional partner fees, statutory disbursements, and GST.
- **Formula:** $\text{Gross Collections} = \sum \text{Amount Received in } \texttt{BILLING-REG} \text{ across all payment receipts}$
- **Source:** `BILLING-REG` cross-checked against Bank Reconciliation | **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 4. Net Service Revenue (NSR)
- **Definition:** Total operational revenue earned by CCS, excluding pure-agent statutory disbursements and pass-through partner professional fees.
- **Formula:** $\text{NSR} = \text{Gross Collections} - \text{Statutory Disbursements (At Actuals)} - \text{Partner Professional Execution Fees} - \text{Output GST}$
- **Source:** `BILLING-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 5. Direct Delivery Costs (DDC)
- **Definition:** Total direct out-of-pocket costs incurred to deliver client engagements, including internal drafter labor allocations, partner sign-off fees, DSC hardware tokens, courier, and specialized search fees.
- **Formula:** $\text{DDC} = \sum (\text{Partner Fees} + \text{Direct Labor Hours} \times \text{Hourly Cost} + \text{Direct Transaction Expenses})$
- **Source:** `BILLING-REG` and `MATTER-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 6. Contribution per Service (CPS) & Contribution Margin (CM)
- **Definition:** Net operating profit generated by each individual service line after deducting direct delivery costs.
- **Formula:** $\text{CPS} = \text{Net Service Revenue}_{\text{service}} - \text{Direct Delivery Costs}_{\text{service}}$  
  $$\text{Contribution Margin \%} = \left( \frac{\text{CPS}}{\text{Net Service Revenue}_{\text{service}}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Source:** Service-level cost accounting in `BILLING-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 7. Outstanding Receivables & Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- **Definition:** Total uncollected invoiced balances and average collection latency.
- **Formula:** $\text{DSO} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Accounts Receivable}}{\text{Total Billed Service Revenue}} \right) \times 30$
- **Source:** `BILLING-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

### C. Delivery Quality & Operational Velocity Metrics

#### 8. On-Time Completion Rate (OTCR)
- **Definition:** Percentage of matters completed and delivered within the published or contractually committed Service Level Agreement (SLA).
- **Formula:** $\text{OTCR} = \left( \frac{\text{Matters Delivered Within Target Turnaround}}{\text{Total Matters Delivered}} \right) \times 100$
- **Source:** `MATTER-REG` (`Filing Date - Intake Completion Date`) | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 9. Rework & Resubmission Rate (RRR)
- **Definition:** Percentage of portal submissions that receive a statutory resubmission query from MCA, RoC, or IP India due to defects or technical objections.
- **Formula:** $\text{RRR} = \left( \frac{\text{Filings Subject to Statutory Resubmission Notice}}{\text{Total Submissions}} \right) \times 100$
- **Source:** `MATTER-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 10. Customer Complaint Rate (CCR)
- **Definition:** Number of formal client dissatisfaction complaints per 100 completed engagements.
- **Formula:** $\text{CCR} = \left( \frac{\text{Formal Escalated Client Complaints}}{\text{Total Completed Engagements}} \right) \times 100$
- **Source:** `MATTER-REG` Issue Log | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

### D. Client Retention & Retainer Health

#### 11. Repeat Customer Rate (RCR)
- **Definition:** Percentage of clients who contract for a second statutory service (e.g. GST or Trademark post-incorporation) within 90 days of formation.
- **Formula:** $\text{RCR} = \left( \frac{\text{Clients with } \ge 2 \text{ Engagements in } \texttt{MATTER-REG}}{\text{Total Active Clients in } \texttt{CLIENT-REG}} \right) \times 100$
- **Source:** `CLIENT-REG` | **Frequency:** Quarterly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 12. Monthly Retainer Revenue (MRR)
- **Definition:** Recurring annualized secretarial or compliance subscription revenue divided by 12.
- **Formula:** $\text{MRR} = \sum \text{Monthly Retainer Fees in } \texttt{BILLING-REG}$
- **Source:** `BILLING-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `₹0 (Pre-Retainer Phase)`

### E. Founder Independence & Operational Scalability

#### 13. Founder Hours per Engagement (FHE)
- **Definition:** Total hours expended by the business founder per completed matter across all stages.
- **Formula:** $\text{FHE} = \frac{\sum \text{Founder Hours Logged on Matter Across Period}}{\text{Total Matters Executed in Period}}$
- **Source:** Time-tracking log tied to `MATTER-REG` | **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Baseline Status:** `TO BE MEASURED`

#### 14. Founder Independence Ratio (FIR)
- **Definition:** Percentage of client engagements completed from initial inquiry to final handover with zero founder intervention.
- **Formula:** $$\text{FIR} = \left( \frac{\text{Completed Engagements with Founder Hours } = 0}{\text{Total Completed Engagements}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Source:** `MATTER-REG` cross-referenced with Founder Timesheets | **Frequency:** Monthly / Quarterly
- **Baseline Status:** `CURRENT STATE: 0% (Foundational setup phase; founder handles or oversees all matters)`

## 3. The 90-Day Empirical Baseline Measurement Protocol

Rather than setting theoretical quotas, CCS adopts a 90-day empirical baseline measurement protocol:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   90-DAY EMPIRICAL BASELINE TIMELINE                   │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┤
│ Month 1 (Days 1-30)│ Month 2 (Days 31-60)│ Month 3 (Days 61-90)          │
├───────────────────┼───────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Zero Quotas     │ • Velocity Review │ • Compute Empirical Baselines  │
│ • Rigorous Logging│ • Identify Delays │ • Establish 12-Month Targets   │
│ • Register Audit  │ • Track Rework    │ • Publish First Executive KPI  │
└───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Protocol Rules:**
   - All 5 operational registers must be maintained continuously for every live inquiry.
   - Exact hours spent by founder and staff must be logged per matter.
   - In Month 4, the observed 90-day medians will become the official corporate performance baselines.
