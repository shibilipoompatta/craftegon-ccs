Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory electronic reporting of corporate social responsibility policies, committee meetings, net profit calculations, 2% expenditure allocation, ongoing projects, and unspent fund transfers to statutory Schedule VII funds.

Statutory Mandate: Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies (CSR Policy) Rules, 2014

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Every company having net worth ≥ ₹500 Crores, turnover ≥ ₹1,000 Crores, or net profit ≥ ₹5 Crores during the immediately preceding financial year.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Calculation of Section 198 net profit for the immediately preceding 3 financial years
  • CSR Committee meeting minutes, approved CSR policy, and Annual CSR Action Plan
  • Details of CSR projects implemented (ongoing vs other than ongoing, geographic locations, partners)
  • CSR Registration Number (Form CSR-1) of all implementing agencies engaged
  • Details of unspent CSR amounts and bank account details for Unspent CSR Account opened under Section 135(6)
  • Audited financial statements and Board Report containing CSR Annexure

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Compute exact 2% average net profit CSR obligation under Section 135(5) and Section 198
  2. Step 2: Classify expenditures into Ongoing Projects vs Single-Year Projects; verify CSR-1 certification of NGOs
  3. Step 3: Transfer any unspent ongoing funds to 'Unspent CSR Account' in scheduled bank within 30 days of FY close
  4. Step 4: Complete Form CSR-2 web-form on MCA V3; input project details, impact assessments, and fund transfers
  5. Step 5: Pre-scrutinize form, download PDF, and affix Class 3 DSC of Director and Practicing Professional
  6. Step 6: Upload Form CSR-2 to MCA V3 portal as linked form with Form AOC-4 or as standalone filing

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to spend or transfer unspent CSR funds attracts severe civil penalty under Section 135(7): Company liable to twice the unspent amount or ₹1 Crore (whichever is less); every defaulting officer liable to 1/10th of unspent amount or ₹2 Lakhs (whichever is less).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can excess CSR expenditure in a year be set off against future obligations?
Yes. Excess amounts spent over the mandatory 2% threshold may be set off against CSR spending requirements for up to the immediately succeeding 3 financial years per Rule 7(3).
Is impact assessment mandatory for all CSR projects?
Impact assessment by an independent agency is mandatory for companies with an average CSR obligation of ₹10 Crores or more in the 3 preceding financial years, for projects with outlays of ₹1 Crore or more.
