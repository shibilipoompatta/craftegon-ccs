Your Business Infrastructure Partner

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) builds dependable operational, secretarial, and regulatory infrastructure for startups, growing enterprises, and cross-border businesses across India.

Our Mission

Transforming Regulatory Friction into Predictable Infrastructure

For Indian entrepreneurs and corporate leaders, statutory compliance has historically been defined by anxiety: opaque government portals (MCA V3, GST, IP India), compounding daily late fees, and fragmented advisors who disappear when an adjudication notice arrives.

CraftEgon Corporate Services was established on a single principle: Compliance should operate like reliable utility infrastructure. You turn it on, it runs accurately in the background, and your founding team stays focused on product development, hiring, and revenue generation.

We do not sell one-off "cheap certificates" or conceal government stamp duties behind misleading headlines. Every filing is handled with upfront statutory fee separation, clear document scoping, and dedicated professional scrutiny.

Operating Architecture

How CraftEgon CCS Operates

A three-tier operating framework uniting technology-enabled procedural accuracy with qualified human professional oversight.

🏛️

Direct Secretarial Execution

We execute non-litigation statutory filings directly across 15 practice verticals—from entity incorporation (SPICe+, FiLLiP) and annual RoC returns (AOC-4, MGT-7) to GST registrations and trademark submissions.

⚖️

Qualified Professional Oversight

All statutory declarations and certifications are reviewed and certified by qualified Company Secretaries in Practice (PCS / ACS / FCS) and Chartered Accountants as required under Section 7 and Section 149 of the Companies Act, 2013.

📊

Transparent Cost Architecture

Zero surprise fees. We enforce a strict visual and accounting distinction between our professional filing assistance fees and statutory government treasury challans / state stamp duties.

Regulatory Boundaries

Compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961

Clear Demarcation: Secretarial Infrastructure vs. Courtroom Advocacy

In strict adherence to the Advocates Act, 1961 and the regulatory standards prescribed by the Bar Council of India, CraftEgon Corporate Services does not provide courtroom appearances, tribunal pleadings, or contentious litigation advocacy.

  • What CCS Delivers: Non-contentious corporate secretarial documentation, RoC and MCA e-filings, statutory register maintenance, tax compliance procedural assistance, and pre-litigation documentation audit.
  • Contentious Litigation Referral: When a matter escalates to formal tribunal dispute (NCLT, NCLAT, High Court, or Income Tax Appellate Tribunal), formal advocacy must be conducted by independent practicing advocates engaged on a separate vakalatnama.
