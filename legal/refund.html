Commercial Terms & Cancellation Standard

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Governing Rules: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

Institutional Governance & Counsel Boundary Disclosure:

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) provides corporate secretarial, regulatory compliance, and statutory documentation infrastructure under Indian corporate law. In compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India regulations, CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) does not provide courtroom appearances, tribunal advocacy, or contentious litigation representation before the NCLT, ITAT, or High Courts. Formal legal representation and tribunal pleadings must be conducted by independent practicing advocates engaged directly on a separate vakalatnama.

1. Tiered Professional Fee Cancellation Matrix

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) operates on a transparent, milestone-driven resource commitment model. When an engagement is booked, professional time is immediately reserved across Practicing Company Secretaries (PCS), Chartered Accountants, and corporate legal specialists. Cancellations of professional fee components are governed strictly by the execution stage of the engagement:

Milestone Tier Cancellation Window Professional Fee Refund Accounting Basis
Tier 1: Pre-Allocation Within 2 hours of payment, prior to specialist assignment 100% Refund Minus 2.5% standard payment gateway transaction charge.
Tier 2: Post-Allocation After specialist assignment & preliminary conflict check, before drafting 80% Refund 20% retained towards KYC verification, conflict screening, and dossier setup.
Tier 3: Active Drafting Statutory instruments, resolutions, or webforms actively drafted 50% Refund 50% retained towards professional drafting hours expended by qualified personnel.
Tier 4: Uploaded / Filed Form uploaded to MCA / GSTN / IP India or SRN challan generated 0% (Non-Refundable) Statutory deliverable fully discharged and submitted to Government portal.

2. Pure Agent Escrow & Statutory Government Disbursements

In accordance with Rule 33 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Rules, 2017, CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) acts strictly as a Pure Agent of the client when receiving advances towards statutory government fees, RoC filing challans, MCA late fees, state stamp duties, and Trade Marks Registry examination charges:

  • Fiduciary Custody: All statutory government outlays are held in a designated client escrow ledger separate from operational professional revenues.
  • 100% Pre-Challan Refund: If an engagement is terminated prior to the generation of the official government challan or payment gateway transmission, 100% of all unspent statutory outlays are immediately returned to the client without deductions.
  • Zero Post-Challan Refund: Once an official Service Request Number (SRN), MCA PayGov challan, GST portal CPIN/CIN, or IP India transaction receipt has been generated, statutory funds are irrevocably transferred into the Consolidated Fund of India or respective State Government treasury. These disbursements are strictly non-refundable by CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS).
  • Zero Commercial Markup: CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) levies zero markup, zero commission, and zero transaction premium on statutory government fees. All government challans are delivered to the client at exact statutory face value.

3. Service Quality Guarantee & Defect Remediation

Every statutory deliverable undergoes multi-tier pre-scrutiny by a qualified Practicing Company Secretary or Chartered Accountant. In the event of a regulatory query, defect notice, or resubmission requirement:

  • Errors Attributable to CCS: If a resubmission notice or defect is raised by the Registrar of Companies (CRC/RoC), GST authorities, or Trade Marks Registry due to an internal drafting error, clerical omission, or procedural fault of CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS), all remediation, re-drafting, and re-filing work will be executed at ₹0 additional professional fee.
  • Errors Attributable to Client Inputs: If a resubmission or rejection arises from false information, altered documents, forged identity proofs, unverified OTP delays exceeding statutory limits, or failure to disclose material facts by the client, rectifications are billed at standard hourly rates or discounted filing fees.

4. Government Portal Outages & Force Majeure

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) maintains active uptime monitoring across MCA V3, GSTN, ICEGATE, and IP India portals. However, CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) cannot be held liable for statutory late fees, filing rejections, or processing delays arising exclusively from:

  • Prolonged downtime, session crashes, or V3 login lockouts of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs portal.
  • Bank payment gateway failures on Bharatkosh, PayGov, or State e-Treasury platforms.
  • Adjudication delays within government departments or judicial tribunal registries.

In all such events, CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) logs formal technical helpdesk tickets with the respective government nodal agency (e.g., MCA Service Desk) and provides contemporaneous proof of timely submission attempts.

5. Refund Settlement Timeline & Method

Approved refunds are processed in strict accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment settlement guidelines:

  • Credit Timeline: Refunds are initiated within 2 business days of approval and typically credit back to the source account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Source Instrument Settlement: Under anti-money laundering (PMLA 2002) guidelines, refunds are strictly remitted to the original payment source instrument (credit card, net banking account, or UPI handle). Cash refunds or third-party bank transfers are prohibited.

6. Statutory Grievance Redressal Mechanism

In compliance with Rule 5(3) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) has appointed a designated Grievance Redressal Officer to address all client escalations, cancellation disputes, and refund reviews:

7. How to Initiate a Cancellation Request

To request a cancellation, submit an email to corporate.secretarial@craftegon.com with the subject line "Cancellation Request: [Invoice / Order Number]", including:

  1. Original payment invoice or transaction reference ID.
  2. Entity name and registered email address.
  3. Specific service name and reason for cancellation.
