## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Fiduciary Boundaries

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) operates as a coordinated business infrastructure platform. To ensure compliance with Indian professional statutes, CCS strictly maintains the legal boundary between **technology-enabled administrative coordination** and **reserved statutory acts**.

### Governing Professional Statutes:
1. **Chartered Accountants Act, 1949:** Statutory audits under Section 139 and tax audits under Section 44AB are strictly reserved for practicing Chartered Accountants holding a valid Certificate of Practice (COP) and registered Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN).
2. **Company Secretaries Act, 1980:** Secretarial audits under Section 204 and annual return certifications under Section 92(2) are strictly reserved for practicing Company Secretaries holding an active COP and peer-reviewed UDIN.
3. **Advocates Act, 1961:** Representation before judicial and quasi-judicial tribunals (NCLT, NCLAT, High Courts) is strictly reserved for enrolled advocates acting on direct, independent client vakalatnamas. CCS does not solicit litigation or act as an advocate.
4. **Trade Marks Act, 1999:** Formal filing and prosecution of trademark applications before the Trademark Registry must be conducted by an authorized Registered Trademark Agent or Advocate.

## 3. Professional Partner Onboarding & Vetting Protocol (The 12-Point Checklist)

Every practicing professional (Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, Advocate, Trademark Agent) must satisfy the following 12 vetting gates before handling client matters:

1. [ ] **Active Regulatory Standing:** Verification of active Certificate of Practice (COP) directly via the ICAI / ICSI / Bar Council public register.
2. [ ] **Disciplinary Check:** Formal declaration confirming zero pending disciplinary proceedings, suspensions, or disqualifications under applicable Institute regulations.
3. [ ] **Peer Review Certification:** For Company Secretaries certifying MGT-7 or MR-3, verification of valid ICSI Peer Review Board certification.
4. [ ] **Digital Signature Security:** Valid Class 3 DSC registered on MCA V3, Income Tax, and IP India portals.
5. [ ] **UDIN Issuance Protocol:** Written agreement to generate and append valid Unique Document Identification Numbers (UDIN) within statutory timeframes.
6. [ ] **Turnaround Time (SLA) Agreement:** Contractual commitment to a 24-to-48 hour turnaround on standard form certifications upon receipt of complete dossiers.
7. [ ] **Conflict of Interest Checks:** Obligation to declare conflicts of interest prior to accepting client matters.
8. [ ] **Non-Solicitation Covenant:** Strict 24-month non-solicitation clause prohibiting direct commercial solicitation of CraftEgon clients.
9. [ ] **Confidentiality & Data Protection:** Strict non-disclosure agreement compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
10. [ ] **Secure IT Infrastructure:** Minimum standard of commercial antivirus, encrypted client document storage, and multi-factor authentication on professional portals.
11. [ ] **Professional Indemnity (Errors & Omissions):** Confirmation of professional indemnity coverage or agreement on liability allocation.
12. [ ] **Written Fee Schedule:** Bilaterally signed rate card detailing fixed fees per form, resubmission policies, and monthly settlement terms.

## 4. Partner Contingency & Failover Protocol

To ensure uninterrupted service delivery:
1. **Dual-Partner Architecture (Primary & Secondary):** For every core filing category (MCA and IP India), CCS must maintain at least one Primary Partner and one pre-vetted Secondary Partner.
2. **SLA Breach Threshold:** If the Primary Partner fails to review or execute a statutory filing within 24 hours of dossier dispatch, the matter is automatically reassigned to the Secondary Partner.
3. **Client Notice:** Clients are notified immediately of any professional re-allocation affecting certification.
