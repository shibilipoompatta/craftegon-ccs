Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What are the core obligations of a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023? +
Under Sections 5 and 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, every Data Fiduciary must give itemized notice in clear and plain language stating specific purposes, personal data categories, grievance redressal mechanisms, and obtain verifiable, unconditional consent before processing.
What is the mandatory reporting timeline for cybersecurity incidents to CERT-In? +
Under the CERT-In Cyber Security Directions (issued under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000), all service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporate, and government organizations must report identified cybersecurity incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them.
