Frequently Asked Questions

What is the relationship between an MSA and an SOW?
The MSA governs the overarching legal, confidentiality, and liability terms between the parties, while individual SOWs define specific project scopes, timelines, deliverables, and fees.
Can an MSA include an arbitration clause under Indian law?
Yes. An arbitration clause naming the seat, venue, and rules under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 ensures fast, confidential resolution of commercial disputes outside public civil courts.
