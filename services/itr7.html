ITR-7 Filing for Companies, Trusts & Institutions

Mandatory annual income tax return submission for institutional, non-profit, political, and exempt entities claiming statutory deductions and tax holidays under Sections 11, 12, 10, and 13A, reporting application of revenue and maintaining non-profit validity.

Statutory Authority

Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory annual income tax return submission for institutional, non-profit, political, and exempt entities claiming statutory deductions and tax holidays under Sections 11, 12, 10, and 13A, reporting application of revenue and maintaining non-profit validity.

Statutory Mandate: Section 139(4A), 139(4B), 139(4C), 139(4D) of the Income-tax Act, 1961

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Charitable and religious trusts (Section 139(4A)), political parties (139(4B)), scientific research associations, news agencies, universities, hospitals (139(4C)), and universities/colleges (139(4D)).

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Audited annual financial statements and statutory audit report in Form 10B / 10BB / 10BA
  • Registration Certificate under Section 12AB, 10(23C), or 80G with active URN
  • Schedule of voluntary contributions, corpus receipts, and anonymous donations
  • Detailed statement of application of income towards institutional objects in India (85% rule)
  • Form 9A / Form 10 for accumulation of funds (if 85% expenditure threshold not met)
  • Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate of authorized trustee / principal officer

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: File prerequisite statutory audit reports (Form 10B/10BB) at least 30 days prior to ITR-7 filing
  2. Step 2: Log in to Income Tax e-Filing Portal (incometax.gov.in) with trust/entity PAN credentials
  3. Step 3: Download ITR-7 offline utility or access online web form; fill Part A General and Schedules
  4. Step 4: Complete Schedule AI, Schedule ER, Schedule EC, and Schedule J (application of funds)
  5. Step 5: Run JSON validation and schema integrity check; verify tax payable/refundable is nil or correct
  6. Step 6: Digitally sign with authorized trustee DSC Class 3; submit and download ITR-V acknowledgement

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Late filing after due date (31st October) attracts late filing fee under Section 234F (up to ₹5,000) and interest under Section 234A. Crucially, late filing completely invalidates exemptions under Sections 11 & 12, subjecting total receipts to 30% tax plus surcharge and cess.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if a trust fails to apply 85% of its income during the year?
The trust can file Form 9A (for deemed application) or Form 10 (accumulating funds up to 5 years for a specific purpose) before the ITR-7 deadline to safeguard exemption.
Is DSC mandatory for filing ITR-7?
Yes. For trusts, political parties, and Section 8 companies, ITR-7 must be electronically signed using a valid Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate.
