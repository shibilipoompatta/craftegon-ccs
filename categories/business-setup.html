CATEGORY 01

Business Setup & Entity Structuring

Establish the right corporate structure for investment, liability limitation, and long-term tax efficiency. Supervised by qualified Company Secretaries under MCA V3 rules.

Select Your Target Entity Structure

Private Limited Company

The gold standard for startups and scaling businesses. Limited liability, separate legal entity status, and equity fundraising readiness via MCA SPICe+.

View Private Limited Scope →

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Combines limited liability with organizational flexibility and lower annual ROC compliance overhead. Ideal for professional consultancies and joint ventures.

View LLP Registration Scope →

One Person Company (OPC)

Single-founder corporate entity offering complete equity control, limited liability, and distinct legal identity without mandatory partner requirements.

View OPC Registration Scope →

Section 8 Non-Profit Company

Formed for non-profit charitable, educational, or environmental objectives. Eligible for 12A/80G tax exemptions and institutional grant funding.

View Section 8 Scope →

Partnership Firm Registration

Registration under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 with Registrar of Firms (RoF), partnership deed drafting, and firm PAN/TAN allocation.

View Partnership Scope →

Foreign Subsidiary & Branch Office

RBI, FEMA, and MCA Form FC-1/FC-3 regulatory coordination for overseas corporations entering the Indian market.

View Foreign Entity Scope →
