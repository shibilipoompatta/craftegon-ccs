Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) Statutory Guide

Statutory overview of creditor petitions under Section 7 and Section 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016. CraftEgon CCS provides corporate documentation support only; formal NCLT representation is conducted exclusively by independent legal counsel.

Statutory Notice & Legal Framework: CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial, compliance, and document drafting infrastructure. CraftEgon CCS does not enter appearances, handle contentious litigation, or provide formal tribunal advocacy before courts or tribunals. Clients requiring legal representation must engage an independent advocate enrolled under the Advocates Act, 1961.

Informational Guide • External Referral Only

Independent Legal Representation Required

CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial, statutory compliance, and document compilation support only. Pursuant to the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India regulations, corporate compliance advisory entities do not enter appearances, plead, or provide contentious tribunal advocacy.

Corporate debtors or creditors requiring formal legal representation before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) must engage an independent practicing advocate for replies, appearances, and tribunal arguments.

Referral Notice: CraftEgon CCS maintains no in-house legal advocacy service, advocate panel agreement, or exclusive referral destination in this repository. External representation must be retained independently through counsel of the client's choice.
