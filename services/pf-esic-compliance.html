Statutory Mandate: Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 & Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

EPFO is mandatory for establishments employing 20 or more persons (voluntary for smaller firms). ESIC is mandatory for establishments employing 10 or more persons (20 in certain states) located in notified areas with employee wages up to ₹21,000/month.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, or Business Registration Proof
  • Entity PAN, GSTIN, and cancelled cheque of active business bank account
  • Rent agreement / proof of premise ownership along with latest utility bill
  • PAN, Aadhaar, and mobile number of directors, partners, or authorized primary signatory
  • Comprehensive employee wage register (gross salary, basic, DA, HRA, and allowances)
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC Class 3) of authorized signatory for EPFO portal approval

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Access Unified Shram Suvidha Portal (shramsuvidha.gov.in); register employer account
  2. Step 2: Submit integrated application for EPFO code and ESIC establishment code with entity data
  3. Step 3: Register Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) on EPFO Unified Employer Portal
  4. Step 4: Generate Universal Account Numbers (UAN) and ESIC IP numbers for all eligible employees
  5. Step 5: Prepare monthly Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) text file with wage and contribution breakdowns
  6. Step 6: Upload ECR, generate TRRN challan, and remit monthly PF/ESI dues online on or before 15th of each month

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Late monthly remittance (beyond 15th of following month) triggers penal damages under Section 14B of EPF Act (scaling from 5% to 25% per annum) and mandatory penal interest under Section 7Q at 12% per annum. Employers deducting employee share without depositing face criminal prosecution under Section 406/409 of IPC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the statutory deadline for depositing monthly PF and ESIC contributions?
Monthly PF and ESIC contributions must be deposited on or before the 15th of the succeeding month via the respective government portals.
Can an establishment register voluntarily under PF even with fewer than 20 employees?
Yes. Establishments with fewer than 20 employees can apply for voluntary registration under Section 1(4) of the EPF Act upon mutual agreement.
