Professional Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2026 • Bar Council of India & Ministry of Corporate Affairs Standard

Mandatory Counsel Boundary & Representation Framework:

CraftEgon CCS provides AI-driven corporate secretarial, compliance automation, and documentation infrastructure. All formal legal representation, tax litigation, and appearances before the NCLT, ITAT, or High Courts are executed exclusively through our panel of independent associated advocates, chartered accountants, and senior legal counsel under the Advocates Act, 1961.

1. No Unsolicited Legal Solicitation or Formal Retainer

The content, guides, statutory knowledge blueprints, calculators, and automated drafting tools provided on the CraftEgon CCS platform are for informational, operational, and educational corporate governance purposes only. Accessing our portals, executing preliminary risk diagnostics, or submitting intake forms does not create an advocate-client, chartered accountant-client, or company secretary-client relationship until a formal, signed client retainer agreement is executed.

2. AI Diagnostics & Computational Estimators

Outputs generated by our interactive calculators (including RoC Section 403 late fee estimators, GST ITC models, Section 115BAC tax comparisons, and equity dilution simulators) represent algorithmic mathematical estimates based on current published statutory schedules. Because official MCA stamp duties, RoC filing fees, and municipal levies depend on state-specific statutory schedules and nominal capital slabs, outputs are indicative and must be validated through formal professional review.

3. Independent Advocacy & Tribunal Boundary

CraftEgon CCS strictly disclaims any role as an attorney-at-law or courtroom litigator. In compliance with the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India norms, all judicial pleadings, writs, petitions under Sections 241/242, insolvency defense under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016, and appearances before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT), or High Courts are conducted solely through independent enrolled advocates engaged on separate professional vakalatnamas.
