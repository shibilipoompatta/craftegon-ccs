Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What is the applicability threshold for mandatory CSR under Section 135 of Companies Act 2013? +
Every company having net worth of ₹500 Crore or more, or turnover of ₹1,000 Crore or more, or net profit of ₹5 Crore or more during the immediately preceding financial year must spend at least 2% of the average net profits made during the three immediately preceding financial years on CSR.
What happens to unspent CSR funds at the end of the financial year? +
If unspent funds relate to an ongoing project, they must be transferred within 30 days to an "Unspent CSR Account" in a scheduled bank and spent within 3 financial years. If not relating to an ongoing project, they must be transferred to a Fund specified in Schedule VII (e.g. PM National Relief Fund) within 6 months.
