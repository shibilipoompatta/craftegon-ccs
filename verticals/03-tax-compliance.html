Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

When is Form 3CEB Transfer Pricing Accountant Report mandatory? +
Under Section 92E of the Income Tax Act, 1961, every person who has entered into an international transaction or specified domestic transaction with Associated Enterprises (AEs) exceeding statutory thresholds must obtain and electronically file Form 3CEB verified by a Chartered Accountant by October 31st.
What is the threshold for mandatory GST annual audit reconciliation in Form GSTR-9C? +
Every registered taxpayer whose aggregate annual turnover exceeds ₹5 Crores during a financial year is mandated to file a self-certified reconciliation statement in Form GSTR-9C along with the annual return in Form GSTR-9 under Section 44 of the CGST Act.
