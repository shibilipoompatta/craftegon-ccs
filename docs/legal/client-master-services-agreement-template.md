### CLAUSE 1: SCOPE OF SERVICES & TRIPARTITE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

1.1 **Scope of Platform Services:** CCS shall provide business administration, secretarial compilation, compliance calendaring, draft documentation, and government portal data preparation services as mutually agreed in writing or booked through the official CCS digital platform (`ccs.craftegon.com`).

1.2 **Statutory Tripartite Architecture:** The Client acknowledges that Indian law reserves specific professional monopolies to qualified statutory practitioners. To maintain strict statutory compliance:
- **Platform Layer (CCS / CRAFTEGON LLP):** Provides technology, operational coordination, document drafting, unassisted portal filing (using Client DSC/credentials), and Pure Agent fee management.
- **Chartered Accountancy & Secretarial Attestation:** All statutory audit reports (Sections 139–143 Companies Act, 2013), tax audit reports (Section 44AB Income Tax Act, 1961), SPICe+ professional declarations (Section 7(1)(b)), and annual return certifications (Section 92(2) Form MGT-8) are performed strictly by independent practicing Chartered Accountants (PCA) or practicing Company Secretaries (PCS) holding valid Certificates of Practice (COP) and generating verifiable **ICAI / ICSI Unique Document Identification Numbers (UDIN)**.
- **Advocacy & Tribunal Representation:** Courtroom litigation, writ petitions, and appearances before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT), or High Courts are executed solely by independent enrolled advocates engaged under a separate, direct **Vakalatnama** in accordance with the Advocates Act, 1961.

---

### CLAUSE 2: ADVOCATES ACT BOUNDARY & LITIGATION DISCLAIMER

2.1 **Non-Litigation Standard:** In compliance with Sections 29, 30, and 33 of the Advocates Act, 1961 and Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules, CRAFTEGON LLP is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation, litigation advocacy, or courtroom pleadings before any court, tribunal, or judicial body.

2.2 **No Corporate Law Practice or Fee-Sharing:** No portion of the fees paid by the Client to CCS constitutes a fee for legal advocacy or courtroom representation. In contested matters (such as Section 9/11 trademark show-cause hearings, trademark oppositions, Section 241/242 oppression petitions, or Section 7/9 IBC insolvency defenses), CCS introduces the Client to an independent empanelled advocate. Privity of contract for litigation exists strictly between the Client and the Advocate under a direct Vakalatnama, with zero fee-splitting or commissions earned by CRAFTEGON LLP.

---

### CLAUSE 3: INDEPENDENT PROFESSIONAL ATTESTATION & UDIN GOVERNANCE

3.1 **Individual Capacity:** All statutory declarations, pre-scrutiny certificates, and audit schedules are signed by empanelled practicing professionals in their individual or firm capacity under their personal professional registration numbers.

3.2 **UDIN Validation:** Every statutory document delivered to the Client that requires CA or CS attestation shall carry a verifiable UDIN. The Client has the right and obligation to verify the authenticity of the UDIN on the official ICAI (`udin.icai.org`) or ICSI (`udin.icsi.edu.in`) verification portals. CRAFTEGON LLP does not issue statutory audit or secretarial opinions in a corporate capacity.

---

### CLAUSE 4: CLIENT DATA ACCURACY, KYC WARRANTIES & STATUTORY FRAUD INDEMNITY

4.1 **Client Warranty of Authenticity:** The Client warrants and represents that all corporate resolutions, identity documents, PANs, Aadhaar cards, bank statements, utility bills, title deeds, and beneficial ownership declarations provided to CCS are genuine, authentic, complete, and lawful.

4.2 **Statutory Penalties under Companies Act, 2013:** The Client acknowledges that providing false, misleading, or forged instruments to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the Central Registration Centre (CRC), the Income Tax Department, or the GST Network constitutes a criminal offense punishable under:
- **Section 447 of the Companies Act, 2013:** Imprisonment for a term not less than 6 months (up to 10 years) and fine up to 3 times the amount involved in fraud.
- **Section 448 of the Companies Act, 2013:** Punishment for false statements or intentional omissions in any return, report, certificate, or e-form.
- **Section 449 of the Companies Act, 2013:** Punishment for false evidence given upon oath or in any affidavit.

4.3 **Full Indemnification:** The Client agrees to unconditionally defend, indemnify, and hold harmless CRAFTEGON LLP, its designated partners, employees, and empanelled professional partners from and against any statutory fines, penalties, show-cause notices, legal expenses, investigations, or civil liabilities resulting from inaccurate, fraudulent, or forged information furnished by the Client.

---

### CLAUSE 5: PURE AGENT STATUTORY DISBURSEMENTS (RULE 33 CGST RULES, 2017)

5.1 **Dual-Component Billing Structure:** Every engagement fee is structured into two distinct legal components:
- **Component A: CCS Professional Service Fees:** Fixed consideration for document drafting, secretarial verification, and filing facilitation, billed with applicable Goods and Services Tax (18% GST, SAC 9983).
- **Component B: Statutory Government Disbursements:** Exact pass-through levies (including MCA filing fees, RoC additional late fees under Section 403, State Stamp Duty, Trade Marks Registry statutory fees under Form TM-A, and PAN/TAN processing charges).

5.2 **Pure Agent Escrow Mechanics:** In strict accordance with **Rule 33 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Rules, 2017**:
- CRAFTEGON LLP acts as a "Pure Agent" of the Client when remitting statutory outlays to government portals (MCA, Bharatkosh, State Stamp Treasuries, IP India).
- All government disbursements are collected into a dedicated, non-interest client statutory disbursement account (`CRAFTEGON LLP - Client Statutory Escrow A/c`).
- Disbursements are remitted strictly at actual cost with zero markup, interest, or commission.
- Official government challan receipts (carrying unique MCA SRN, Bharatkosh Transaction ID, or IP India CBR numbers) are generated in the Client's legal name and provided as documentary proof.
- Statutory disbursements are 100% exempt from GST on CCS tax invoices.

### CLAUSE 6: DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT, 2023 COMPLIANCE

6.1 **Affirmative Processing Consent:** In compliance with Sections 5 and 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the Client consents to the collection, storage, and processing of personal identifiers (names, PANs, Aadhaar numbers, DINs, residential addresses, and biometric/DSC details) by CRAFTEGON LLP strictly for executing authorized statutory registrations and regulatory filings.

6.2 **Data Fiduciary Obligations:** CRAFTEGON LLP shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect client data against unauthorized access, loss, or leakage. Client data shall be shared exclusively with official government registries and empanelled professionals under statutory non-disclosure obligations.

6.3 **Statutory Retention Exception:** In accordance with Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Section 44AA of the Income Tax Act, 1961, corporate records, accounting ledgers, and filed statutory returns shall be retained in encrypted storage for a mandatory statutory period of eight (8) years, notwithstanding any general data erasure request.

---

### CLAUSE 7: SERVICE LEVELS, PORTAL DOWNTIME & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 **CCS Turnaround SLA:** CCS guarantees that all document drafting, internal quality verification, and professional pre-scrutiny shall be completed within 48–72 business hours following receipt of complete, verified KYC documentation and required signatures from the Client.

7.2 **Registry Portal Downtime Exclusion:** The Client acknowledges that statutory approvals, corporate name reservations, DIN allotments, Certificate of Incorporation (CoI) issuance, and trademark examination reports are issued at the sole statutory discretion of government officers. CCS shall not be held liable for delays or missed filing deadlines caused by:
- Technical outages, gateway errors, or scheduled maintenance on government registries (MCA V3, GSTN, ITD, IP India).
- Unreasonable delays by the Client in signing digital signature tokens (DSC), providing OTP verifications, or paying statutory challans.
- Official queries, resubmission orders, or show-cause notices issued by regulatory authorities.

7.3 **Aggregate Monetary Liability Cap:** In all circumstances and across all causes of action (whether in contract, tort, negligence, or statutory indemnity), the maximum aggregate financial liability of CRAFTEGON LLP shall be strictly capped at the **total professional service fees actually paid by the Client to CCS for the specific engagement giving rise to the claim**, excluding government disbursements and third-party fees. Under no circumstances shall CRAFTEGON LLP be liable for any indirect, consequential, punitive, or loss-of-profit damages.

### CLAUSE 8: GOVERNING LAW, JURISDICTION & DISPUTE ARBITRATION

8.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement and all rights and obligations arising hereunder shall be governed by, interpreted, and construed in accordance with the substantive and procedural laws of the Republic of India.

8.2 **Binding Arbitration:** Any dispute, difference, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, validity, breach, or termination, shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered under the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996** (as amended).
- The arbitral tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the Parties. If the Parties fail to agree upon a sole arbitrator within 30 days of a notice of dispute, the appointment shall be made in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
- The seat and legal venue of arbitration shall be **Kozhikode, Kerala, India**.
- The arbitration proceedings shall be conducted in the English language, and the arbitral award shall be final, binding, and enforceable across all jurisdictions.

8.3 **Supervisory Court Jurisdiction:** Subject to the arbitration clause above, the courts of competent jurisdiction located at **Kozhikode, Kerala** shall have exclusive supervisory jurisdiction over any judicial proceeding relating to this Agreement.
