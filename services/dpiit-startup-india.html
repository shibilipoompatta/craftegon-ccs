Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Operating without recognition forfeits 80-IAC tax holidays and subjects share premium to Angel Tax scrutiny. If a recognized startup invests in prohibited assets under Para 4 of GSR 127(E), recognition is revoked retrospectively with 200% penalty under Section 270A.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an existing partnership firm or proprietorship apply for DPIIT recognition?
No. Only registered Private Limited Companies, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), and Registered Partnerships can obtain DPIIT recognition.
Is Section 80-IAC tax holiday granted automatically with DPIIT certificate?
No. DPIIT recognition is granted first. A separate application is reviewed by the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) for the 3-year 100% corporate tax holiday under Section 80-IAC.
