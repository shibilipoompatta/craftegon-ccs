Section 241 & 242 Oppression and Mismanagement Statutory Guide

Statutory guide on shareholder remedies against mismanagement, fund diversion, asset stripping, and illegal share dilution under Sections 241–242 of the Companies Act, 2013. Statutory Notice & Legal Framework: CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial review, statutory register reconstruction, and forensic accounting compilation under the Companies Act, 2013. Under the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India regulations, formal legal representation, contentious petition drafting, and appearances before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) or NCLAT under Sections 241–242 are conducted exclusively by independent practicing advocates retained directly by the client. CraftEgon CCS does not provide courtroom appearances or tribunal advocacy.

Track 1 • Direct Corporate Secretarial Advisory (CCS) Companies Act, 2013

Secretarial Audit & Forensic Record Compilation

For shareholders, founders, or boards addressing allegations of oppression, prejudice, or mismanagement, CraftEgon CCS conducts comprehensive statutory audits and records verification:

  • Section 244 Eligibility Verification: Auditing shareholder qualifying thresholds (minimum 100 members or 1/10th of total members, or members holding ≥ 10% of issued share capital).
  • Statutory Register Inspection & Reconstruction: Detailed forensic review of the Register of Members (MGT-1), Register of Directors & KMP (MBP-1), and Register of Loans & Investments (MBP-2).
  • Minutes & Resolution Trail Auditing: Verification of board and general meeting notices, quorum compliance, and voting minutes under Section 118.
  • Historical Share Allotment Audit: Cross-referencing Form PAS-3 allotment filings, valuation reports, and share certificate stamping for unauthorized dilution.

Track 2 • Contentious Tribunal Litigation (Independent Counsel) Advocates Act, 1961

Formal NCLT Company Petition Representation

Filing or defending a petition for relief against oppression and mismanagement under Section 241 or seeking interim relief under Section 242(4) requires independent practicing legal counsel:

  • Petition Drafting: Preparing the formal company petition in Form NCLT-1, accompanying affidavits in Form NCLT-4, and vakalatnama authorization must be executed by practicing advocates.
  • Interim Injunctions & Hearings: Seeking or opposing interim orders (e.g. restraining board resolutions, superseding directors, or freezing share transfers) and courtroom arguments before NCLT benches cannot be conducted by corporate secretarial advisory firms.
  • Client Selection: Representation must be retained independently through qualified legal counsel of the client's choice. CraftEgon CCS maintains no in-house litigation advocacy service, exclusive advocate referral agreements, or legal fee sharing.
