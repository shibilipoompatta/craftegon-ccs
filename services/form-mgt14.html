Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory filing of special resolutions passed by shareholders, agreements, and specified board resolutions with the ROC within 30 days of passing.

Statutory Mandate: Section 117, Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 24 of Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Any company passing special resolutions (change of name, registered office, objects, capital increase) or Section 179(3) board resolutions.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Certified true copy of Special Resolution / Board Resolution with explanatory statement
  • Copy of notice issued to shareholders convening the general meeting
  • Copy of altered constitutional documents (MoA / AoA / Scheme)
  • Class 3 DSC of Director and practicing professional (CS/CA)

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Pass special resolution at general meeting with required 3/4th majority
  2. Step 2: Draft certified copy of resolution with detailed explanatory statement under Section 102
  3. Step 3: Access Form MGT-14 web-form on MCA V3 within 30 days of the resolution date
  4. Step 4: Attach resolution copy, meeting notice, and altered MoA/AoA
  5. Step 5: Affix Director and PCS DSCs; submit on portal and pay statutory fees

Frequently Asked Questions

Are private companies required to file MGT-14 for Section 179(3) board resolutions?
No. Private companies are exempt from filing MGT-14 for routine Section 179(3) board powers (e.g. borrowing, investing, approving accounts) per MCA Notification G.S.R. 464(E).
What happens if MGT-14 is not filed within 30 days?
Late fees apply. In certain cases, condonation of delay from the Regional Director (RD) under Section 87 may be required.
