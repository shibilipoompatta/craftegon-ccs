RBI Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) Return Blueprint

Comprehensive regulatory walkthrough of Reserve Bank of India (RBI) reporting obligations under FEMA, 1999 for Indian entities with foreign direct investments (FDI) or overseas investments.

2. Statutory Deadlines & Reporting Portal

  • Submission Platform: Online reporting via the dedicated RBI FLAIR portal (Foreign Liabilities and Assets Information Reporting).
  • Due Date: July 15th of every year (based on provisional financial accounts if audited accounts are unavailable by the deadline).
