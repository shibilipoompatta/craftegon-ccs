CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) is an integrated business infrastructure and corporate compliance services firm operating out of Room No.2, NS Building, Kaanjippuzha, Alappuzha, Kerala, India (PIN 690501). ### A.4 The Six Lifecycle Stages
CCS structures its client engagements across six distinct entity lifecycle stages:
1. **ESTABLISH:** Entity selection, jurisdictional analysis, corporate formation (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, Section 8), and foundational statutory tax registrations (PAN, TAN, GST, MSME Udyam).
2. **OPERATE:** Post-incorporation compliance, initial board resolutions, bank current account opening coordination, PF/ESIC registrations, and statutory register initialization.
3. **COMPLY:** Recurring annual corporate governance, RoC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7/7A), DIR-3 KYC director verification, auditor appointment (ADT-1), and GST returns.
4. **PROTECT:** Intellectual property protection (Form TM-A trademark clearance), commercial contract architecture (Founder Pacts, MSAs, SOWs), and employment policy compliance (POSH Act, Labour Codes).
5. **GROW:** Corporate restructuring, capital expansion (PAS-3), ESOP scheme secretarial execution, Section 233 fast-track mergers, and cross-border FEMA compliance.
6. **CONTINUE / CONCLUDE:** Orderly corporate maintenance, dormant status applications (MSC-1), or fast-track voluntary strike-off and entity dissolution (STK-2 / LLP-24).

#### Detailed Operational Specifications for the Core 8:

#### 1. Private Limited Company Incorporation (SPICe+ / MCA V3) — `V01-SVC-007`
- **Target Audience:** Startups, technology ventures, high-growth scalable enterprises.
- **Statutory Authority:** Companies Act 2013 (Section 2(68)); SPICe+ (INC-32), e-MOA (INC-33), e-AOA (INC-34), AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35).
- **Core Deliverables:** Name Approval (RUN/Part A), Certificate of Incorporation (CoI), Corporate Identity Number (CIN), 2 Director Identification Numbers (DIN), Digital Signature Certificates (DSC Class 3), Company PAN & TAN, EPFO/ESIC registration codes, Profession Tax (state-specific), and pre-activated corporate current bank account.
- **Professional Requirement:** Certification by an independent Practicing CA or CS on Form INC-32.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 5 to 7 business days from complete KYC receipt.

#### 2. Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Registration — `V01-SVC-002`
- **Target Audience:** Professional services, consultancies, family businesses, bootstrapped service firms.
- **Statutory Authority:** Limited Liability Partnership Act 2008; RUN-LLP, FiLLiP, Form 3.
- **Core Deliverables:** RUN-LLP Name Approval, Certificate of Incorporation, LLPIN, 2 DPINs, Class 3 DSCs, LLP PAN & TAN, custom-drafted LLP Agreement executed and filed in Form 3 within 30 days.
- **Professional Requirement:** Certification by an independent Practicing CS, CA, or Advocate on Form FiLLiP.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 7 to 10 business days.

#### 3. Sole Proprietorship & Partnership Setup — `V01-SVC-005` / `V01-SVC-006`
- **Target Audience:** Individual traders, regional service providers, traditional retail businesses.
- **Statutory Authority:** Indian Partnership Act 1932; State Shops & Commercial Establishments Acts.
- **Core Deliverables:** Custom Partnership Deed drafting, Stamp Paper notarization coordination, Form 1 Registrar of Firms (RoF) submission, Partnership PAN/TAN, MSME Udyam registration certificate.
- **Professional Requirement:** Corporate legal drafting by internal team; state sub-registrar liaison.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 3 to 5 business days.

#### 4. MSME Udyam & Tax Registration Coordination — `V01-SVC-003` / `V03-SVC-003`
- **Target Audience:** Small enterprises seeking statutory government benefits, priority lending, and Section 43B(h) payment protections.
- **Statutory Authority:** MSMED Act 2006; Income Tax Act 1961; CGST Act 2017.
- **Core Deliverables:** Official Udyam Registration Certificate with verification QR badge, GSTIN registration certificate, PAN/TAN delivery.
- **Professional Requirement:** Internal administrative coordination; GST verification by tax specialist.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 2 to 4 business days.

#### 5. Trademark Search & Form TM-A Filing Coordination — `V06-SVC-003`
- **Target Audience:** Businesses launching unique brand names, logos, slogans, or product packaging.
- **Statutory Authority:** Trade Marks Act 1999; Trade Marks Rules 2017.
- **Core Deliverables:** Pre-search phonetic and visual conflict analysis across NICE Classes 1–45; formal Form TM-A drafting and electronic filing on the IP India portal; official filing receipt with TM Application Number allowing immediate use of the ™ symbol.
- **Professional Requirement:** Execution by an enrolled Registered Trademark Agent or Advocate.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 24 to 48 hours for filing receipt post client approval.

#### 6. RoC Annual Compliance Package — `V02-SVC-017`
- **Target Audience:** Operating Private Limited companies seeking to avoid director disqualification and ₹100/day MCA late fees.
- **Statutory Authority:** Companies Act 2013; Sections 92, 137, 139, 149.
- **Core Deliverables:** Preparation of Financial Statements filing (Form AOC-4), Annual Return (Form MGT-7/7A), Annual Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC Web/eForm for all directors), Statutory Auditor Appointment verification (Form ADT-1), Board Resolutions for AGM approval, and Director Disclosures (MBP-1 & DIR-8).
- **Professional Requirement:** Certification by a Practicing Company Secretary (PCS) on Form MGT-7; Statutory Audit balance sheet certification by independent Statutory Auditor (CA).
- **SLA & Turnaround:** Staged throughout annual statutory calendar (September–November).

#### 7. Standard Commercial Contracts Pack — `V05-SVC-002` / `V05-SVC-001`
- **Target Audience:** Startups and service businesses onboarding clients, vendors, or equity co-founders.
- **Statutory Authority:** Indian Contract Act 1872; Information Technology Act 2000.
- **Core Deliverables:** Custom-drafted Master Service Agreement (MSA) with limitation of liability, Statement of Work (SOW) template, Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), Founder Equity Vesting and Non-Compete Agreement.
- **Professional Requirement:** In-house corporate legal drafting; lawyer review.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 3 to 5 business days per contract draft.

#### 8. Fast-Track Company Strike-Off (Form STK-2) — `V02-SVC-013`
- **Target Audience:** Defunct, inactive, or uncommenced companies with zero assets and zero liabilities wishing to close cleanly.
- **Statutory Authority:** Companies Act 2013 (Section 248); Companies (Removal of Names of Companies from the Register of Members) Rules 2016.
- **Core Deliverables:** Statement of Accounts (Form STK-8) drafting, Director Affidavits (Form STK-4) and Indemnity Bonds (Form STK-3), Special Resolution / EGM documentation, Form STK-2 e-Filing with Registrar of Companies.
- **Professional Requirement:** Mandatory certification of Statement of Accounts by a Practicing Chartered Accountant (FCA); certification of Form STK-2 by Practicing CS.
- **SLA & Turnaround:** 15 to 30 days to RoC gazette publication; final dissolution notice per MCA timeline.

## SECTION F: Professional Partner Onboarding & Governance

### F.1 Regulatory Background (`LEGAL/PROFESSIONAL VALIDATION REQUIRED`)
Under Indian law:
- **Chartered Accountants Act 1949:** Statutory audits under Section 139/141, Tax Audits under Section 44AB, and Transfer Pricing reports under Section 92E can only be conducted by a Chartered Accountant in Practice holding a valid Certificate of Practice (CoP) and Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN).
- **Company Secretaries Act 1980:** Secretarial Audits under Section 204 and annual return certifications under Section 92(2) can only be conducted by a Practicing Company Secretary (PCS) holding a valid CoP and peer-reviewed firm credentials.
- **Advocates Act 1961:** Representation and pleading before courts and judicial tribunals (NCLT, NCLAT, High Court) are reserved strictly for enrolled Advocates. Fee-sharing or corporate partnership with non-advocates for litigation is prohibited.

### F.2 Partner Onboarding Protocol (12-Point Checklist)
Before any external professional is assigned a client matter:
1. **Identity & Credential Verification:** Certified copy of Membership Certificate, Certificate of Practice (CoP), and active disciplinary standing certificate from ICAI, ICSI, or Bar Council.
2. **Firm Structure & Experience:** Proof of minimum 3 years active practice in corporate filings or commercial law.
3. **UDIN Compliance Guarantee:** Formal undertaking to generate genuine UDINs for all statutory certifications within prescribed timelines.
4. **Professional Indemnity Insurance:** Recommended coverage for professional negligence.
5. **Standard Service Agreement:** Subcontracting agreement defining commercial terms, turnarounds, and deliverable standards.
6. **Strict Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality:** Binding NDA protecting all client trade secrets and proprietary data.
7. **DPDP Act 2023 Data Processing Addendum:** Data fiduciary-to-processor agreement mandating strict technical safeguards.
8. **Client Non-Solicitation Covenant:** 24-month strict non-solicitation of clients introduced by CCS.
9. **Conflict of Interest Disclosure:** Mandatory affirmative declaration prior to accepting any specific matter.
10. **SLA & Turnaround Commitment:** Agreement to respond to assigned draft reviews within 24–48 hours.
11. **Quality Review Acceptance:** Agreement to submit filings to CCS internal quality verification before portal submission.
12. **Disciplinary & Escalation Procedure:** Documented grounds for immediate de-empanelment and report to institute disciplinary directorates in case of ethical violations.
