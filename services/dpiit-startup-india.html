Boundary: DPIIT Certificate ≠ Sec 140 Tax Holiday

Securing DPIIT Recognition unlocks GeM tender waivers and IPR rebates, but does NOT automatically grant the 3-year 100% tax holiday. Under Section 140 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (formerly Sec 80-IAC), only Private Limited Companies & LLPs with separate IMB Certification qualify. Partnership Firms & Cooperatives cannot claim Sec 140.

Startup India Framework Under G.S.R. 108(E) (2026) & KSUM

1. Standard vs Deep Tech Thresholds

Standard startups qualify up to 10 years & ₹200 Cr turnover (doubled from ₹100 Cr). Dedicated Deep Tech Startups qualify up to 20 years & ₹300 Cr turnover, accommodating long R&D gestation cycles across AI, biotech, semiconductors, and robotics.

2. Section 140 (80-IAC) & IMB Board

100% profit deduction for any 3 consecutive tax years out of 10 (up to ₹300 Cr turnover; subject to 14% MAT). Requires formal evaluation of innovation/scalability and pitch review before the Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB).

3. IPR Rebates & GeM Procurement

Unlocks an 80% Patent fee discount (plus govt-borne SIPP facilitator fees and expedited examination) and a 50% Trademark rebate (₹4,500 vs ₹9,000), alongside GeM prior-turnover/experience tender waivers.

4. Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) & SISFS

State grants in Kerala via KSUM Unique ID: equity-free Idea Grant (up to ₹3L) and Productisation/Scale-up grants (up to ₹7–10L+), complementing Central SISFS (up to ₹50L via incubators) and CGSS collateral-free debt.

Frequently Asked Legal & Tax Questions

Can a Sole Proprietorship or One Person Company (OPC) register as a DPIIT Startup?

A Sole Proprietorship is strictly ineligible for DPIIT Startup India recognition under G.S.R. 108(E). However, a One Person Company (OPC) is legally a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013 and therefore IS eligible for DPIIT recognition. Similarly, LLPs, Registered Partnerships (Sec 59), and Cooperative Societies qualify under G.S.R. 108(E).

Why are DPIIT applications rejected, and how can rejection be avoided?

Most rejections stem from: (1) generic trading or consulting descriptions failing to articulate technological/process innovation; (2) businesses formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing enterprise; or (3) missing supporting evidence (working URL, prototype deck, or technical architecture). Crafting a precise innovation narrative tailored to DPIIT scrutiny metrics resolves most queries.

Does DPIIT recognition exempt our startup from ROC or annual tax compliance?

No. DPIIT-recognized startups remain fully subject to regular statutory filings under the Companies Act, 2013 (AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC, INC-20A) or LLP Act, 2008 (Form 11, Form 8), GST returns, TDS compliance, RBI FC-GPR filings for FDI, and applicable DPDP Act data standards.
