Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What is the filing timeline for Form FC-GPR upon receipt of FDI under FEMA regulations? +
An Indian company issuing capital instruments to a person resident outside India must report the transaction in Form FC-GPR on the RBI FIRMS (Foreign Investment Reporting and Management System) portal within 30 days from the date of allotment of equity shares or CCPS.
What is the penalty for delayed reporting of foreign investment in FIRMS? +
Late reporting attracts Late Submission Fees (LSF) calculated on a matrix based on the amount involved and period of delay under RBI Master Direction on Reporting, or compounding proceedings under Section 15 of FEMA 1999.
