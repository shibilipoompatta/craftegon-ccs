Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What is the statutory duration of trademark registration in India under Form TM-A? +
Under Section 25 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999, registered trademarks remain valid for an initial period of 10 years from the date of filing and can be perpetually renewed every 10 years by filing Form TM-R.
Can software source code be protected under Indian Intellectual Property law? +
Yes, software source code and object code are protected as Literary Works under Section 2(o) of the Copyright Act, 1957. While software per se is not patentable under Section 3(k) of the Patents Act, software integrated with novel hardware or demonstrating technical effect can be patented.
