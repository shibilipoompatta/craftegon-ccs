Common Operational Bottlenecks

Why Registration Alone Isn't Operational Readiness

Exporters routinely hit expensive port demurrages after incorporation because IEC registration lacks port-wise Bank AD Code mapping, GST zero-rated LUT setup, cold-storage LSGD/KSPCB consents, or carrier/CHA SLA contracts covering temperature excursions and customs holds.

PREMISES & FACILITY CHECKS Site-Dependent Scope

Port, Bonded Godown & Cold-Storage Premises Readiness

  • Ensure Bank Authorized Dealer (AD) Code registration is mapped on ICEGATE per port (Cochin/Vallarpadam, Beypore, or air cargo complexes) before shipping bills are generated.
  • Add every warehouse and cold-storage location cleanly as an Additional Place of Business under GSTIN alongside K-SWIFT/LSGD godown permissions.
  • Review KSPCB refrigerant/noise thresholds and commodity RCMC rules (Spices Board, APEDA, MPEDA, or FIEO) based on cargo classification.

Regulatory & Registration Scoping: Coordination and dossier preparation; statutory approvals rest solely with the competent authorities.

DGFT-ICEGATE Core Filing Area

DGFT IEC, ICEGATE & Port-Wise AD Code Registration

Authority: DGFT / ICEGATE Customs Portal / Authorized Dealer Bank

IEC registration, ICEGATE entity mapping, and bank Authorized Dealer (AD) Code port linkage readiness prior to first Bill of Lading or Shipping Bill.

GST-LUT-REF Core Filing Area

GST Registration & Annual Export LUT (Bond-Free Export)

Authority: Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN)

GSTIN setup with warehouse/godown additional places of business and Letter of Undertaking (Form GST RFD-11) for zero-rated exports without IGST cash blockage.

COMMODITY-RCMC Activity / Threshold Based

Spices Board / APEDA / MPEDA / FIEO RCMC Routing

Authority: Respective Export Promotion Councils & Commodity Boards

Commodity-specific Registration-Cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) documentation and FSSAI Import/Export clearance readiness where perishables or food consignments apply.

WAREHOUSE-KL Activity / Threshold Based

K-SWIFT, LSGD Godown License & KSPCB Cold-Storage Consent

Authority: K-SWIFT / Kerala LSGD / KSPCB / Fire & Rescue

Warehouse trade licensing, cold-room/chiller KSPCB noise & refrigerant thresholds, and fire safety NOC checklists for cold-chain facilities.

FEMA-INBOUND Activity / Threshold Based

FDI / Cross-Border Capital Structuring (FC-GPR & FLA Readiness)

Authority: Reserve Bank of India (FIRMS / FLAIR Portal)

Structuring NRI/foreign equity inflows under FEMA rules, share valuation coordination, and initial RBI reporting setup.

Organizing Phase 1 entity and pre-launch dossiers alongside Phase 2 CraftEgon LLP commercial agreements and Phase 3 recurring compliance helps prevent operational delays caused by disconnected vendors.

Are all listed registrations, contracts, or tax exemptions automatically included in every quote? +

No. Each item listed in this sector framework depends on your exact business activity, premises location, capital structure, headcount, and current laws. Exact scope is determined through diagnostic assessment and confirmed in writing before work begins.
