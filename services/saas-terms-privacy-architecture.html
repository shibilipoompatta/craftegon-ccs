Frequently Asked Questions

How can a SaaS company ensure clickwrap agreements are legally binding in India?
Under Section 10A of the Information Technology Act, electronic contracts entered into via electronic means (e.g., clicking 'I Agree' during signup) are fully recognized and enforceable in Indian courts if clear notice and consent logging are implemented.
What should be the limitation of liability cap in enterprise SaaS agreements?
Standard industry practice caps the vendor's aggregate commercial liability to the total subscription fees paid by the customer in the preceding 12 months, excluding breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence.
