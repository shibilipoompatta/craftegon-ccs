Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

Which companies are required to obtain a Secretarial Audit Report in Form MR-3? +
Under Section 204(1) of the Companies Act 2013 read with Rule 9 of the Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules 2014, secretarial audit is mandatory for: (a) every listed company, (b) every public company with paid-up capital of ₹50 Crore or more, or turnover of ₹250 Crore or more, or outstanding loans/borrowings exceeding ₹100 Crore.
Who is authorized to issue Form MR-3? +
Only an independent Company Secretary in Whole-time Practice (PCS) holding a valid Certificate of Practice (COP) from the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) can sign and certify Form MR-3.
