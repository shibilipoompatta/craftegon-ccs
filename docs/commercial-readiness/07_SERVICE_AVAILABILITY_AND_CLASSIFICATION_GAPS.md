## 1. Classification Methodology

To ensure absolute legal defensibility under the Advocates Act, 1961, the Companies Act, 2013, the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, and the Company Secretaries Act, 1980, every one of the **57 approved canonical corporate services** on the CraftEgon platform is categorized into one of three distinct delivery tiers:

1. **Tier 1: Direct Administrative & Processing Support (Internal Desk)**
   - Operational tasks, portal applications, data entry, document formatting, and government coordination that do not legally require statutory certification by a member of ICAI, ICSI, or Bar Council.
   - *Delivery:* Executed directly by CraftEgon corporate services staff.
2. **Tier 2: Professional-Led Services (Practising CS / CA / TM Agent Panels)**
   - Statutory filings, audits, valuations, and corporate certifications where government portals mandate professional credentials, Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) attestation, or Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN).
   - *Delivery:* Coordinated by CraftEgon; certified and submitted by empaneled Practising Company Secretaries, Chartered Accountants, or registered Trademark Agents.
3. **Tier 3: Specialist Legal Referral (Independent Bar Council Advocates)**
   - Contentious litigation, court/tribunal appearances (NCLT, NCLAT, High Court), drafting of adversarial pleadings, and formal legal advocacy.
   - *Delivery:* Referred directly to independent advocates. CraftEgon accepts zero referral fee and provides no legal representation.

## 3. Operational Availability Gaps & Owner Action Items

Before launching aggressive paid marketing or customer acquisition campaigns, the business owner must resolve four operational dependencies:

1. **Bilateral Partner Capacity Agreements:**
   - Formalize written retainer and capacity agreements with at least two (2) Practising Company Secretaries (PCS) and two (2) Chartered Accountants (CA) in Kozhikode or Kerala to guarantee 24-hour turnaround for SPICe+ Part B and DIR-3 KYC filings.
2. **Statutory Fee Capital Reserve:**
   - Establish a dedicated operational bank balance of ₹25,000 to pre-fund MCA V3 challan payments and IP India fees immediately upon client authorization, preventing filing delays.
3. **Turnaround Time Realism:**
   - Ensure marketing communications never promise "Incorporation in 24 Hours". Because MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC) processing times fluctuate between 2 to 7 working days, set realistic client expectations (3–5 working days for Pvt Ltd; 1–2 days for Udyam).
4. **Advocate Panel Documentation:**
   - Maintain a vetted roster of independent High Court and NCLT advocates to ensure rapid referral handoffs for dispute inquiries without creating regulatory friction.
