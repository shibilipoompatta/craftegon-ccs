Statutory Mandate: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act)

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Every employer having 10 or more employees across all branches, offices, or remote units in India. Mandatory for private companies, public companies, NGOs, and IT establishments.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Headcount report across all workplace locations (including contractors, interns, and remote staff)
  • Senior woman employee profile designated as Presiding Officer
  • Two employee members committed to the cause of women or possessing legal knowledge
  • Engagement agreement with qualified External Member (from NGO or legal specialist familiar with POSH)
  • Draft workplace anti-sexual harassment policy and inquiry procedure rules
  • Record of sensitization sessions and annual inquiry logs for District Officer reporting

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Execute formal Board Resolution and Office Order constituting the Internal Committee (IC)
  2. Step 2: Onboard qualified, independent External Member and establish inquiry secretariat
  3. Step 3: Draft and circulate institutional POSH Policy aligned with the 2013 Act and Supreme Court guidelines
  4. Step 4: Conduct mandatory employee awareness workshops and specialized training for IC members
  5. Step 5: Prepare Annual POSH Report under Section 21 detailing complaints received, resolved, and pending
  6. Step 6: Submit Annual Report to jurisdictional District Officer (Local Complaints Committee / WCD) by 31st January

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to constitute an Internal Committee or contravention of POSH Act provisions attracts a statutory fine of ₹50,000 on the employer. Repeat violations attract double the fine and mandatory cancellation or non-renewal of business licenses and incorporation registrations by the government.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is POSH compliance mandatory if an enterprise has fewer than 10 employees?
While an Internal Committee (IC) is not required for fewer than 10 employees, the employer must still comply with basic anti-harassment provisions; complaints are handled by the District Local Committee (LC).
Does the annual POSH report need to be disclosed in the MCA Board Report?
Yes. Under Section 134 read with the Companies (Accounts) Rules, every company must include a mandatory disclosure in its Annual Board Report confirming compliance with the constitution of the Internal Committee.
