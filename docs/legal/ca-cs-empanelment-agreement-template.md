## 2. Institutional Context & Code of Ethics Compliance
1. **Independent Professional Judgment:** Nothing in this Agreement shall compromise, restrict, or influence the statutory independence or professional judgment of the Empanelled Professional under the regulations of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) or the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).
2. **Mandatory UDIN Generation:** All statutory certifications, attestation of incorporation forms (SPICe+ Part B), annual returns (Form MGT-7/7A, Form 8), and audit reports (Form 3CEB) must be accompanied by a verifiable **Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN)** generated by the Empanelled Professional on the official ICAI or ICSI portal.
3. **Prohibition of Fee-Sharing:** Compensation paid by CRAFTEGON LLP to the Empanelled Professional is strictly an institutional B2B professional fee for verification, review, and digital certification. No fee-sharing or commission is paid on statutory audit fees.

---

## 3. Scope of Professional Allocation
CRAFTEGON LLP will allocate client engagements based on domain expertise and geographical convenience across:
* **SPICe+ Part B Certification:** Review of subscriber identities, Registered Office proof, and execution of professional declaration under Section 7(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013.
* **FiLLiP LLP Incorporation:** Professional verification under Rule 18(2) of LLP Rules, 2009.
* **Annual Secretarial Certification:** Form MGT-7/7A, Form AOC-4 XBRL review, and Annual Solvency Statements (Form 8).
* **Tax Audit & Transfer Pricing Dossiers:** Form 3CEB international transaction verification under Section 92E of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

---

## 4. Turnaround Times (SLAs) & Professional Standards
* **Initial Scrutiny:** Within twenty-four (24) business hours of receiving the complete client file and KYC dossier from CRAFTEGON LLP.
* **Attestation & Signing:** Within twelve (12) business hours of final client approval and successful MCA/DGFT portal generation.
* **Queries & Defects:** Any drafting defect, name reservation conflict, or regulatory resubmission requirement must be flagged with precise statutory citations to the CRAFTEGON compliance desk.

---

## 5. Confidentiality & DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance
1. All client documents, DSC PINs, financial statements, and director KYC records are strictly confidential and protected under the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**.
2. The Empanelled Professional shall not retain or use client data for any purpose other than executing the specific statutory mandate.
3. Client poaching or direct solicitation of CRAFTEGON platform clients for independent offline services is strictly prohibited for a period of twenty-four (24) months following engagement.
