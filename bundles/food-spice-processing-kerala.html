Who This Is Built For

Target Business Profiles in Kerala

  • Spice, Coconut, Coffee, Dehydrated Foods & Value-Added Agri Processors across Malabar & Central Kerala
  • D2C Packaged Food, Functional Beverage & Nutraceutical Manufacturing Units
  • Private-Label Contract Packers & Food Brands scaling into Modern Trade and GCC Exports

Kerala's Malabar spice belt, KINFRA food parks, and NRI-funded D2C wellness brands are shifting from raw commodity trading to value-added branded processing, requiring simultaneous factory-level statutory clearance and shelf-ready retail compliance.

Common Operational Bottlenecks

Why Registration Alone Isn't Operational Readiness

Plant founders frequently invest in machinery before realizing LSGD industrial building rules, KSPCB Consent to Establish/Operate (effluent/boiler thresholds), Central vs State FSSAI manufacturing/nutraceutical categories, Legal Metrology packaging declarations, and missing OEM/distributor indemnity contracts can block retail launch.
