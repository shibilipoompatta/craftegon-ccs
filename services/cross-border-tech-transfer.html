Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Detailed description of technology, source code, patent, or proprietary know-how being transferred
  • Licensing parameters (exclusive vs non-exclusive, territorial scope, sub-licensing rights, term)
  • Commercial consideration model (upfront licensing fee, running royalties, milestone payments)
  • Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) and Form 10F of overseas licensor for DTAA treaty benefits
  • Withholding tax (TDS) rate analysis under Section 195 vs applicable Double Tax Avoidance Agreement
  • Authorised Dealer (AD Bank) documentation guidelines for outward foreign exchange remittance

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Conduct transactional tax review to categorize payments (Royalty vs Fees for Technical Services vs Business Profits)
  2. Step 2: Reconcile DTAA treaty benefits and verify Supreme Court Engineering Analysis ruling on software payments
  3. Step 3: Draft Technology Transfer Agreement / Master Software License with comprehensive IP warranties
  4. Step 4: Obtain Tax Residency Certificate (TRC), Form 10F, and No-PE (Permanent Establishment) declaration from foreign licensor
  5. Step 5: Obtain Form 15CA (taxpayer filing) and Form 15CB (Chartered Accountant certification) on Income Tax Portal
  6. Step 6: Submit outward remittance pack to Authorised Dealer (AD) Bank for cross-border foreign exchange transfer

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Frequently Asked Questions

Is royalty paid for standard off-the-shelf foreign software subject to withholding tax in India?
Following the landmark Supreme Court ruling in Engineering Analysis Centre of Excellence, payment by Indian end-users for shrink-wrapped or off-the-shelf software is not taxable as royalty, exempting it from Section 195 TDS when DTAA benefits apply.
What is the requirement for Form 15CA and 15CB?
Form 15CB is a statutory certificate from a Chartered Accountant verifying tax withholding rates, which is then submitted alongside Form 15CA on the Income Tax Portal prior to making an overseas remittance through an AD Bank.
