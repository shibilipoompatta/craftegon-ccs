1. Introduction to MCA V3 Portal & SPICe+ Architecture

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) transitioned corporate registry operations to the advanced V3 Portal, replacing legacy V2 electronic filing systems. The SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically) web-based application serves as the single digital gateway for company formation in India.

Unlike traditional systems, V3 operates on a user-centric profile dashboard requiring individual registration for directors, professionals, and promoters before initiating web forms, ensuring higher data security and streamlined workflow tracking.

2. Part A: Name Approval & Reservation Workflow

SPICe+ Part A is dedicated to name reservation for new companies. Promoters can submit up to two proposed names along with an indicator of core business objects (NIC codes).

  • Phonetic & Trademark Check: Names must not resemble existing trademarks or registered company titles under Rule 8 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.
  • Resubmission Window: If the Registrar of Companies (RoC) issues a deficiency memo, a single resubmission window is provided without incurring fresh government fees.

3. Part B: Comprehensive Incorporation & Linked Services

Once Part A is approved, promoters proceed to SPICe+ Part B, which integrates multiple regulatory services into a unified form, eliminating separate bureaucratic touchpoints:

DIN & DSC Generation

Simultaneous issuance of Director Identification Numbers and Digital Signature validation.

PAN & TAN Issuance

National tax identity numbers allocated concurrently with the Certificate of Incorporation (COI).

EPIC / ESIC & Bank Setup

Mandatory labour registrations and zero-balance current account initiation links.

4. Post-Incorporation Mandate: Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business)

Incorporation alone does not grant immediate operational freedom. Under Section 10A of the Companies Act, 2013, newly incorporated companies having a share capital must file Form INC-20A within 180 days of incorporation.

Key Compliance Requirements for INC-20A:

  • Declaration by directors that every subscriber to the memorandum has paid the value of shares agreed upon.
  • Verification of registered office address filed via Form INC-22.
  • Certification by a practicing Chartered Accountant (CA), Company Secretary (CS), or Cost Accountant (CMA).

Failure to file Form INC-20A within the statutory 180-day window invites heavy financial penalties for the company and officers in default, and can restrict the company from commencing business or exercising borrowing powers.
