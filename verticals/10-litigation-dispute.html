Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What are the eligibility thresholds to file an oppression and mismanagement petition before NCLT? +
Under Section 244 of the Companies Act 2013, members holding not less than one-tenth (10%) of the issued share capital of the company or not less than one-hundred (100) members or one-tenth of total number of members (whichever is less) are entitled to file a petition under Section 241.
Can the NCLT waive the 10% shareholding requirement under Section 244? +
Yes, the proviso to Section 244(1) empowers the Tribunal to waive all or any of the eligibility requirements upon an application made to it, enabling minority shareholders with less than 10% to agitate genuine oppression claims.
