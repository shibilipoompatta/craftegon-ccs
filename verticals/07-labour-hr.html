Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

When is an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) mandatory under the POSH Act 2013? +
Under Section 4 of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2013, every employer of a workplace employing 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Committee (IC) chaired by a senior woman employee with at least 50% women members and an external NGO expert.
What are the penalties for non-constitution of an ICC? +
Non-compliance attracts a statutory fine of up to ₹50,000 for the first offense, and repeated violations can lead to double penalties and potential cancellation or revocation of business licenses or municipal registrations.
