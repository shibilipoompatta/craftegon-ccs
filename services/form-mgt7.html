Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory annual statutory snapshot of the enterprise: shareholding pattern, transfers, promoter holdings, directorships, board meetings, and KMP remuneration.

Statutory Mandate: Section 92, Companies Act, 2013 read with Rule 11 of Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Every company registered under the Companies Act. Small companies and OPCs file simplified Form MGT-7A.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Register of members, debenture holders, and share transfer records as of financial year close
  • List of board meetings, committee meetings, and general meetings held with attendance logs
  • Remuneration details of directors and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP)
  • Details of penalties, fines, or compounding of offenses during the year
  • Form MGT-8 secretarial certification (for companies with paid-up capital ≥ ₹10 Cr or turnover ≥ ₹50 Cr)

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Reconcile annual register of members and calculate shareholding percentages
  2. Step 2: Access Form MGT-7 (or MGT-7A for small firms/OPCs) on the MCA V3 portal
  3. Step 3: Enter meeting counts, director details, and attach shareholder list CSV/PDF
  4. Step 4: Pre-scrutinize web-form; affix Director Class 3 DSC and certifying PCS DSC
  5. Step 5: Submit within 60 days of AGM date and pay fee via Bharatkosh

Government Fees & Disbursements

Base ROC fee: ₹200 to ₹600 based on share capital; Late fee: ₹100 per day flat under Section 403 without upper cap.

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Company liable to penalty of ₹10,000 + ₹100/day up to ₹2,00,000 under Section 92(5); every officer in default liable to ₹10,000 + ₹100/day up to ₹50,000. Contributes to 5-year Director Disqualification under Section 164(2).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Form MGT-7 and MGT-7A?
MGT-7A is a simplified annual return exclusively for One Person Companies (OPCs) and Small Companies, signed solely by a director without requiring PCS certification.
What is the statutory deadline for filing MGT-7?
Within 60 days from the date on which the Annual General Meeting (AGM) was held.
