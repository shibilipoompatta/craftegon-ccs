Turnaround Precision

Company Incorporation Timeline

A typical process may take around 7–10 working days after complete documentation, subject to name availability, government processing, and any resubmission.

STAGE 01

Documentation & DSC

Duration: 1–2 Days

Collection of KYC credentials, Director identification verification, and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) generation.

STAGE 02

Name Reservation

Duration: 1–2 Days

MCA RUN filing for company name clearance and legal uniqueness checks against registered trademark databases.

STAGE 03

SPICe+ Submission

Duration: 3–5 Days

Integrated filing covering MoA, AoA, DIN allocation, PAN/TAN generation, EPFO, ESIC, and statutory banking integration.

STAGE 04

COI & Handover

Duration: 1 Day

Issuance of Certificate of Incorporation, corporate identity kit, bank account setup facilitation, and compliance schedule.
