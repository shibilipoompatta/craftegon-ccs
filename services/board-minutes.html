Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

All companies registered in India (Private Limited, Public Limited, OPC, Section 8) required by law to maintain formal minutes and statutory registers at their registered office.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Details of board meetings held (dates, attendees, mode of meeting: in-person or video conference)
  • Record of matters discussed, resolutions passed, and director dissents or recusals
  • Form MBP-1 general disclosures of interest submitted by all directors
  • Share allotment records, share transfer deeds (Form SH-4), and stamp duty certificates
  • Register of Directors & Key Managerial Personnel (Section 170) with DIN details
  • Register of Loans, Guarantees, Securities and Investments (Section 186) and RPTs (Section 188)

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Draft formal notices and agenda packs with detailed notes for upcoming Board / Committee meetings
  2. Step 2: Record meeting proceedings and draft draft minutes within 15 days of meeting conclusion per SS-1
  3. Step 3: Circulate draft minutes to all board members for comments; integrate feedback
  4. Step 4: Finalize minutes, print on numbered pages in formal Minutes Book, and obtain Chairman signature within 30 days
  5. Step 5: Maintain and update electronic statutory registers (Register of Members, Directors, Charges, Investments)
  6. Step 6: Maintain secure digital repository of signed minutes and certified true copies of resolutions

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Tampering with or failing to maintain minutes in accordance with Section 118 attracts a company fine of ₹25,000 and officer fine of ₹5,000; person found guilty of minute tampering punishable with imprisonment up to 2 years and fine up to ₹1,00,000. Frequently Asked Questions

Can board minutes be maintained purely in electronic format?
Yes. Under the Companies Act, 2013 and SS-1, minutes can be maintained in electronic form with timestamping and digital signatures ensuring tamper-proof integrity.
What is the statutory deadline for entering and signing minutes?
Minutes must be entered in the Minutes Book within 30 days of the conclusion of the meeting and signed by the Chairman of the meeting or the Chairman of the next succeeding meeting.
