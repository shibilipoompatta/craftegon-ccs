Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

A Private Limited Company is India's most recognized corporate structure for venture-backed startups, technology businesses, manufacturing enterprises, and foreign subsidiary establishments seeking limited liability and separate legal personality.

Mandatory Statutory Requirements under Companies Act, 2013:

  • Minimum Directors: At least 2 directors (maximum 15). At least one director must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for ≥ 182 days during the financial year under Section 149(3).
  • Minimum Shareholders: At least 2 shareholders (individuals or corporate bodies, up to a maximum of 200 members under Section 2(68)). Directors can also be shareholders.
  • Minimum Capital: No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement under the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015. Authorized capital can start at ₹1,00,000.
  • Registered Office: A physical commercial or residential address located in India where statutory communications can be received.

Scope of Work & Exclusions

Included in Scope

Statutory Inclusions:

  • Director Identification Number (DIN) allocation for up to 3 directors via SPICe+.
  • Name availability check & reservation via SPICe+ Part A (RUN protocol).
  • Statutory drafting of electronic Memorandum of Association (e-MOA, INC-33) and Articles of Association (e-AOA, INC-34).

Post-Incorporation (Separate)

Subsequent Obligations:

  • Class-3 DSC Tokens: Not included in the package. If you already have a valid DSC, you can use it at no extra cost. If not, you must pay extra to obtain a new DSC.
  • Form INC-20A: Mandatory Commencement of Business declaration within 180 days (available under separate mandate).
  • Form ADT-1: Statutory Auditor Appointment within 30 days of incorporation by the Board.
  • State Stamp Duty: Varies by jurisdiction (e.g., Kerala, Maharashtra, Karnataka) and charged at actuals.
  • Annual RoC Compliance: AOC-4 balance sheet and MGT-7 annual return filings.
  • Trademark / IP Registration: Form TM-A brand protection filed under IPR practice.

11-in-1 Unified Clearances via SPICe+ & AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35)

Under Rule 38 and Rule 38A of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014, a single filing of SPICe+ with AGILE-PRO-S simultaneously secures 11 foundational government approvals and institutional accounts:

1. Company Incorporation (CIN) Form INC-11 Certificate of Incorporation issued by RoC
2. DIN Allotment Director Identification Numbers for up to 3 directors
3. Permanent Account Number (PAN) Direct issuance from CBDT Income Tax Department
4. Tax Deduction Number (TAN) Corporate tax withholding identity for TDS payroll
5. EPFO Provident Fund Code Establishment code for social security compliances
6. ESIC Medical Insurance Employer insurance code under ESI Regulations
7. Professional Tax (PT) Mandatory enrolment (MH, KA, WB, etc.)
8. Corporate Bank Account Board resolution & KYC documentation kit for any client-selected bank (zero bank partnerships)
9. Shops & Establishment Municipal commercial registration (Delhi, MH)
10. GSTIN (Optional) Goods and Services Tax registration upon choice
11. Digital MoA & AoA Electronic charter execution via Form INC-33/34

CRC Manesar Scrutiny Defense & Zero-Resubmission Protocol

Under MCA Rule 38(4), applications examined by the Central Registration Centre (CRC Manesar) are limited to exactly two resubmissions within a strict 15-day window. If defects are not cured, the filing is marked Rejected (NTBR), forfeiting state stamp duty. CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) enforces a multi-point pre-filing audit to ensure zero-defect submissions and eliminate CRC resubmission delays:

✓ Utility Bill < 45 Days Rule

✓ 3-Layer Name Pre-Clearance

Screening across MCA Master Data, IP India Trademark Registry (Class search), and phonetic similarity checks under Rule 8A.

✓ CBDT Identity Sanitization

Letter-for-letter cross-verification of director names and DOBs against Income Tax PAN records before DSC affixation.

✓ Standardized Bilingual Owner NOC

Legally binding consent deed paired with ownership title deed to prevent tenancy scrutiny questions.

Mandatory Statutory Professional Certification (Section 7(1)(b))

Under Section 7(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013, every incorporation filing requires a formal statutory declaration in Form INC-8 certified by an Advocate, Chartered Accountant (FCA/ACA), Cost Accountant (FCMA/ACMA), or Company Secretary in Practice (FCS/ACS) confirming that all requirements of the Act and Rules have been satisfied. CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) coordinates this professional scrutiny and digital certification through qualified corporate secretaries.

Mandatory Post-Incorporation Calendar: Day 0 to Day 180

Incorporation does not grant immediate operational freedom. The Companies Act, 2013 imposes strict statutory milestones within the first 180 days:

DAY 0–30
First Auditor Appointment & 1st Board Meeting (Sec 139 & 173)

The Board must appoint the company's first statutory auditor within 30 days of registration. File Form ADT-1 with RoC. Convene the 1st Board meeting to authorize banking and issue shares.

DAY 0–60
Share Certificates Issuance (Section 56(4)(a))

Physical or demat share certificates must be issued to all founding subscribers within 60 days of incorporation. Pay appropriate state stamp duty on share certificates within 30 days of issuance.

DAY 0–180
Form INC-20A Commencement of Business (Section 10A & Rule 23A)

Mandatory filing before commencing business or borrowing: Each subscriber must remit their capital from their personal bank account. Attach bank statement proving capital credit and registered office photographs.

Statutory Default Penalty: ₹50,000 on company + ₹1,000/day on directors (max ₹1,00,000) + RoC power to strike off company name under Section 248.

Frequently Asked Questions (Incorporation FAQs)

Can a foreign national or Non-Resident Indian (NRI) be a director?

Yes. Any foreign national or NRI can be appointed as a director in an Indian Private Limited Company. However, under Section 149(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, at least one director on the Board must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for at least 182 days during the financial year.

Is a commercial office lease required, or can a residential address be used?

Under MCA regulations, your company's registered office can be a residential premises (such as a director's home or rented apartment), provided you furnish a recent utility bill and an authorized No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner.

What is the statutory minimum paid-up capital required?

Following the Companies (Amendment) Act 2015, the minimum paid-up capital requirement of ₹1,00,000 was abolished. You can incorporate with any nominal paid-up capital (e.g. ₹1,000 or ₹10,000), although standard authorized capital is typically kept at ₹1,00,000 or ₹10,00,000 for startup valuation structuring.

What happens if the CRC issues a resubmission query on our name or filing?

MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC) permits up to 2 statutory resubmissions within 15 calendar days without charging additional government filing fees. CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) reviews and resolves any CRC scrutiny queries promptly to maintain compliance momentum.

What immediate compliances must be completed after receiving the COI?

Within 30 days, the Board must appoint the first statutory auditor (Form ADT-1). Within 180 days, directors must deposit their subscribed capital into the bank account and file Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business) before initiating commercial operations.
