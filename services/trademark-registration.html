Brand Name, Logo & Trademark Registration (Form TM-A)

Secure nationwide legal exclusivity for your commercial brand name, corporate logo, and tagline under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 via the official Trade Marks Registry (CGPDTM). Professional phonetic clearance, multi-class Nice mapping, instant application filing, and immediate authorization to use the ™ symbol.

Statutory Authority

Trade Marks Registry / CGPDTM (DPIIT)

Eligible Applicants

Individuals, Startups, LLPs, Companies

Filing SLA

24 to 48 Hours (Instant ™ Usage Rights)

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Trademark registration is vital for any enterprise investing capital and effort into building brand equity, customer goodwill, or intellectual property. Without a registered mark, competitors can copy your product names, duplicate your logos, or register identical domain names without statutory infringement penalties.

Applicant Categories & Concessional Fee Eligibility:

  • Individual / Sole Proprietor: Eligible for the lower statutory government filing fee of ₹4,500 per class.
  • Startups (DPIIT Recognized): Entities holding a valid DPIIT Certificate of Recognition receive an official 50% concession, paying ₹4,500 per class.
  • MSMEs (Udyam Registered): Enterprises possessing an official Udyam Registration Certificate pay ₹4,500 per class (50% statutory discount).
  • Other Corporate Bodies: Companies, LLPs, or partnerships without MSME/Startup status pay the standard statutory fee of ₹9,000 per class.

Brand Protection Comparison: Registered Trademark vs Unregistered Mark

Protection Dimension Registered Trademark (®) Unregistered Common-Law Mark Mere Company / RoC Name
Geographic Scope Nationwide across all 28 states & UTs Restricted to localized area of actual trade Protects corporate name only, not brands
Statutory Infringement Remedies Direct lawsuit under Section 29 with injunctions Heavy burden to prove passing-off under common law No trademark infringement remedies
Symbol Usage Rights ™ upon filing, ® upon final registration Illegal to use ® symbol (punishable offense) Cannot use ® or ™
Licensing, Franchising & Sale Fully assignable commercial asset (Form TM-P) Difficult to value or monetize Non-transferable standalone name
Validity Period 10 Years (Renewable indefinitely via TM-R) Ceases when trade ceases Subject to active company maintenance

Scope of Work & Exclusions

Included in Scope

Statutory Inclusions:

  • Comprehensive phonetic and wordmark similarity search on the official IP India registry.
  • Multi-class Nice Classification mapping (Classes 1–34 Goods / Classes 35–45 Services).
  • Assessment of distinctiveness and statutory registrability under Section 9 & Section 11.
  • Drafting and preparation of Form TM-A (Wordmark or Device/Logo Mark).
  • Preparation and execution of Form 48 (Power of Attorney).
  • Digital e-filing with Class 3 DSC on the official CGPDTM portal within 24–48 hours.
  • Official Government Filing Receipt with permanent Trademark Application Number.
  • Tracking of application status through examination report generation.
Exclusions & Subsequent Proceedings

Exclusions & Subsequent Obligations:

  • Form TM-M Examination Reply: Formal legal replies to Examination Reports issued by the Registry under Section 9/11 (available under separate mandate).
  • Show Cause Hearing Attendance: Personal or virtual appearance before the Trademark Hearing Officer if marked for hearing.
  • Opposition Defense: Contested third-party opposition proceedings before the Registrar (Section 21) or courtroom litigation.
  • Multiple Classes: Base fee covers 1 single Nice class; additional classes require separate statutory and professional fees.

What You Provide (Applicant Input Checklist)

To file your Form TM-A with zero Registry queries, our IPR desk coordinates these basic inputs:

1. Brand Name & Logo Artwork

Exact brand name spelling, wordmark text, and high-resolution logo artwork (PNG/JPEG) if applying for a visual device mark.

2. User Date & Evidence (If In Use)

Exact date since the mark has been used in India (with invoices/marketing links), or confirmation that it is "Proposed to be used".

3. Concession Certificate (If MSME)

Udyam Registration Certificate or DPIIT Recognition Certificate to unlock the 50% statutory fee waiver (₹4,500 instead of ₹9,000).

Required Documents Checklist

1. Applicant KYC Documents

  • Self-attested PAN card of individual or corporate entity.
  • Identity & Address Proof: Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport.
  • Certificate of Incorporation (COI) / Partnership Deed for corporate applicants.
  • Udyam Certificate or DPIIT letter (for 50% fee concession).

2. Brand & Usage Evidence

  • High-resolution logo image file (if device mark).
  • Signed Form 48 (Power of Attorney authorizing trademark agent).
  • User Affidavit under Rule 25 (if prior use in India is claimed).
  • Invoices, promotional flyers, or domain records supporting use date.

Frequently Asked Questions (Trademark FAQs)

When can I legally start using the ™ symbol?

You can start using the ™ symbol immediately upon filing Form TM-A and receiving the official Trademark Application Number from the Registry. The ® symbol can ONLY be used after the Registry issues the final Certificate of Registration.

How long does it take to get a final Trademark Registration Certificate?

While the ™ rights and application number are obtained within 24–48 hours, the complete statutory process - including examination by the Registrar, advertisement in the official Journal, and the mandatory 4-month public opposition period - typically takes 6 to 12 months.

Should I apply for a Wordmark or a Logo (Device Mark)?

A Wordmark provides the broadest legal protection because it protects the brand name in any style, font, size, or color. A Device Mark protects a specific graphic logo design or stylized artwork. We generally recommend filing a Wordmark first, followed by a Device Mark for key brand symbols.

How long does a trademark registration remain valid in India?

Under Section 25 of the Trade Marks Act, a registered trademark is valid for 10 years from the application filing date. It can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years by filing Form TM-R with the statutory renewal fee.

How does MSME Udyam registration save 50% on government trademark fees?

Under the Trade Marks Rules 2017, the Government of India provides a 50% statutory fee waiver for small enterprises. By attaching a valid Udyam certificate, the statutory filing fee is reduced from ₹9,000 to ₹4,500 per class, creating immediate cost savings.
