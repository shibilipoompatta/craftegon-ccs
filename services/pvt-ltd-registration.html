Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

A Private Limited Company is India's most recognized corporate structure for venture-backed startups, technology businesses, manufacturing enterprises, and foreign subsidiary establishments seeking limited liability and separate legal personality.

Mandatory Statutory Requirements under Companies Act, 2013:

  • Minimum Directors: At least 2 directors (maximum 15). At least one director must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for ≥ 182 days during the financial year under Section 149(3).
  • Minimum Shareholders: At least 2 shareholders (individuals or corporate bodies, up to a maximum of 200 members under Section 2(68)). Directors can also be shareholders.
  • Minimum Capital: No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement under the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015. Authorized capital can start at ₹1,00,000.
  • Registered Office: A physical commercial or residential address located in India where statutory communications can be received.

Scope of Work & Exclusions

Included in Scope

Statutory Inclusions:

  • Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) with encryption for 2 directors.
  • Director Identification Number (DIN) allocation for up to 3 directors via SPICe+.
  • Name availability check & reservation via SPICe+ Part A (RUN protocol).
  • Statutory drafting of electronic Memorandum of Association (e-MOA, INC-33) and Articles of Association (e-AOA, INC-34).
  • SPICe+ Part B incorporation filing with MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC).
  • Integrated AGILE-PRO-S filing: mandatory EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax, and Bank Current Account opening.
  • Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) issuance.
  • Certificate of Incorporation (COI) issued by RoC with Corporate Identity Number (CIN).
Post-Incorporation (Separate)

Subsequent Obligations:

  • Form INC-20A: Mandatory Commencement of Business declaration within 180 days (available under separate mandate).
  • Form ADT-1: Statutory Auditor Appointment within 30 days of incorporation by the Board.
  • State Stamp Duty: Varies by jurisdiction (e.g., Kerala, Maharashtra, Karnataka) and charged at actuals.
  • Annual RoC Compliance: AOC-4 balance sheet and MGT-7 annual return filings.
  • Trademark / IP Registration: Form TM-A brand protection filed under IPR practice.

Frequently Asked Questions (Incorporation FAQs)

Can a foreign national or Non-Resident Indian (NRI) be a director?

Yes. Any foreign national or NRI can be appointed as a director in an Indian Private Limited Company. However, under Section 149(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, at least one director on the Board must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for at least 182 days during the financial year.

Is a commercial office lease required, or can a residential address be used?

Under MCA regulations, your company's registered office can be a residential premises (such as a director's home or rented apartment), provided you furnish a recent utility bill and an authorized No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner.

What is the statutory minimum paid-up capital required?

Following the Companies (Amendment) Act 2015, the minimum paid-up capital requirement of ₹1,00,000 was abolished. You can incorporate with any nominal paid-up capital (e.g. ₹1,000 or ₹10,000), although standard authorized capital is typically kept at ₹1,00,000 or ₹10,00,000 for startup valuation structuring.

What happens if the CRC issues a resubmission query on our name or filing?

MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC) permits up to 2 statutory resubmissions within 15 calendar days without charging additional government filing fees. CraftEgon CCS reviews and resolves any CRC scrutiny queries promptly to maintain compliance momentum.

What immediate compliances must be completed after receiving the COI?

Within 30 days, the Board must appoint the first statutory auditor (Form ADT-1). Within 180 days, directors must deposit their subscribed capital into the bank account and file Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business) before initiating commercial operations.
