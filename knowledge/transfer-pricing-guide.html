Complete Guide to Transfer Pricing and Form 3CEB Compliance in India

Navigating international transactions, associated enterprises, and arm's length pricing requirements under Section 92E of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

1. What is Transfer Pricing?

Transfer pricing refers to the pricing of goods, services, intangibles, or financial transactions transferred between related or associated enterprises (AEs) operating across international borders. Tax authorities scrutinize these transactions to ensure prices reflect fair market value ("arm's length price") rather than profit-shifting strategies.

2. Mandatory Requirement for Form 3CEB

Any enterprise that has entered into international transactions or specified domestic transactions with associated enterprises during the financial year must obtain a report from a Chartered Accountant in Form 3CEB under Section 92E. Failure to furnish this report attracts severe penalties under Section 271G.

3. Key Compliance Steps

  • Identification of Associated Enterprises (holding/subsidiary/common control).
  • Documentation of functional analysis (FUNCOM) and economic characterization.
  • Selection of the most appropriate method (CUP, Resale Price, Cost Plus, TNMM, etc.).
  • Filing of Form 3CEB electronically before the specified due date.
