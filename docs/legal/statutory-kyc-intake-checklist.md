## 1. Quality Invariant & Verification Objective

The objective of this Master KYC & Onboarding Standard is to enforce **100% first-pass acceptance** at government regulatory portals (MCA V3, GSTN, ITD, IP India). Re-submissions at Central Registration Centre (CRC) or statutory show-cause notices increase client cycle times and statutory late fee risks. 

Every document received by CCS must be subjected to the **Three-Point Pre-Filing Diagnostic**:
1. **Name & Identity Consistency:** The spelling of names, parents' names, and dates of birth across PAN, Aadhaar, Passport, and Bank Statement must match character-for-character.
2. **Freshness & Timeliness:** Utility bills and bank statements must be dated strictly **within two (2) calendar months** of the date of e-form filing.
3. **Legibility & Resolution:** Color scans must be crisp, uncompressed (minimum 300 DPI), with all four borders visible, zero watermarks, and no digital filters.

## 3. Rejection Traps & Pre-Filing Quality Rules

Before any e-form is submitted to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs or Trade Marks Registry, the CCS compliance desk must verify that the file does **not** contain any of the following 10 fatal rejection defects:

1. **Utility Bill Aging Defect:** Utility bill is older than 60 days from the date of filing (instant CRC resubmission).
2. **PAN Character Mismatch:** Middle names or initials present on Aadhaar/Passport but missing on the Income Tax PAN database.
3. **Unstamped Bank Statements:** Internet banking statements lacking the bank's digital watermark, branch logo, or account holder name.
4. **Incorrect State Stamp Paper Denomination:** Using fixed ₹100 stamp paper for LLP Agreement or Partnership Deed in states requiring ad valorem duty based on capital contribution (e.g., Kerala Stamp Act).
5. **Missing Rule 25 Exhibits:** Claiming a specific date of first use in Form TM-A while failing to attach a notarized affidavit and dated commercial exhibits.
6. **Incomplete Registered Office Address:** Leaving out police station jurisdiction, taluk, or pin code on municipal electricity bills.
7. **Unregistered DSC on MCA V3:** Attempting to submit SPICe+ Part B without first associating the Director DSC with an active Business User account on the MCA V3 portal.
8. **Missing POSH Clause in Directors' Report:** Omitting the mandatory statement regarding Internal Committee constitution under Section 134(3)(q) of the Companies Act.
9. **Unreconciled GSTR-2B vs. Books:** Submitting GST annual return GSTR-9 with discrepancies between ITC ledger and purchase register.
10. **Expired Driving License or Passport:** Attaching an expired identity document as secondary ID proof for director appointment.
