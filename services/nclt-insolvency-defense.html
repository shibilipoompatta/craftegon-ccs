Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) Statutory Guide

Statutory overview of creditor petitions under Section 7 and Section 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016. CraftEgon CCS provides corporate documentation support only; formal NCLT representation is conducted exclusively by independent legal counsel.

Statutory Notice & Legal Framework: CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial, compliance, and document drafting infrastructure. All formal legal representation, court appearances, and litigation advocacy are handled exclusively through independent associated advocates and senior legal counsel under the Advocates Act, 1961.

Informational Guide • External Referral Only

Independent Legal Representation Required

CraftEgon Corporate Services provides corporate secretarial, accounting reconciliation, and document compilation support. Under the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India regulations, corporate compliance advisory firms do not practice law, enter appearances, or provide contentious tribunal advocacy.

Corporate debtors facing Section 7 or Section 9 petitions before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) must engage an independent practicing advocate for formal representation, replies, and hearing appearances.
