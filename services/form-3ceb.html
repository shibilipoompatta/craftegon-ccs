Form 3CEB International Transfer Pricing Report

Mandatory annual certification by an independent Chartered Accountant verifying that cross-border transactions and specified domestic transactions with Associated Enterprises (AEs) adhere to Arm's Length Pricing (ALP) principles, preventing illicit base erosion and profit shifting.

Why This Statutory Filing Is Required

Mandatory annual certification by an independent Chartered Accountant verifying that cross-border transactions and specified domestic transactions with Associated Enterprises (AEs) adhere to Arm's Length Pricing (ALP) principles, preventing illicit base erosion and profit shifting.

Statutory Mandate: Section 92E read with Rule 10E of the Income-tax Rules, 1962

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Every Indian enterprise that has entered into international transactions (inter-company services, royalties, software delivery, loans, guarantees) or Specified Domestic Transactions (SDTs exceeding ₹20 Crores under Section 92BA) with Associated Enterprises during the financial year.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • Audited financial statements and inter-company ledger accounts of all group entities
  • Master File (Form 3CEAA) and Local File documentation maintained under Rule 10D
  • Inter-company Master Services Agreements (MSA), Transfer Pricing Policies, and invoices
  • Functional, Asset, and Risk (FAR) analysis framework of the Indian operating entity
  • Comparable company screening datasets and database extracts (Prowess / Capitaline)
  • CA Class 3 DSC for electronic portal certification

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Log in to Income Tax e-Filing Portal; navigate to 'Authorised Partners > My CA' and assign Form 3CEB
  2. Step 2: CA compiles transactional values, FAR profile, and economic benchmarking methodologies
  3. Step 3: Select appropriate transfer pricing method (TNMM, CUP, RPM, CPM, PSM, or Other Method)
  4. Step 4: Prepare Annexure to Form 3CEB detailing nature of international transactions and ALP adjustments
  5. Step 5: CA approves, attaches benchmarking certificates, and signs electronically using Class 3 DSC
  6. Step 6: Assessee director/authorized signatory reviews and accepts Form 3CEB on portal before 31st October

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to furnish Form 3CEB report under Section 92E attracts a mandatory flat penalty of ₹1,00,000 (₹1 Lakh) under Section 271BA. Failure to keep/maintain transfer pricing documentation under Section 92D attracts an additional penalty of 2% of transaction value under Section 271AA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the statutory deadline for filing Form 3CEB?
The statutory deadline is 31st October of the relevant assessment year, which is one month prior to the corporate income tax return deadline for entities subject to TP audit.
Is Form 3CEB mandatory if international transactions are below a certain monetary threshold?
There is no monetary de minimis threshold for international transactions with Associated Enterprises; even a ₹1 international transaction mandates Form 3CEB filing.
