MCA Late Fee & Additional Fee Structure Guide

An analytical breakdown of statutory additional fees under Section 403 of the Companies Act, 2013 for delayed filing of annual returns (AOC-4, MGT-7) and event-based forms.

1. The Additional Fee Slab System

When statutory documents are filed beyond their prescribed due dates, the MCA V3 portal automatically levies additional fees calculated as multiples of normal filing fees based on the duration of delay:

Period of Delay Additional Fee Multiplier
Up to 30 days 1x Normal Fee
31 to 60 days 2x Normal Fee
61 to 90 days 4x Normal Fee
91 to 180 days 6x Normal Fee
Beyond 180 days 10x Normal Fee (plus potential prosecution)
