Statutory Compliance Notice • Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Framework

Effective Date: October 2026 • Governing Law: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & Information Technology Act, 2000

Institutional Governance & Counsel Boundary Disclosure:

CraftEgon CCS provides AI-driven corporate secretarial, compliance automation, and documentation infrastructure. All formal legal representation, tax litigation, and appearances before the NCLT, ITAT, or High Courts are executed exclusively through our panel of independent associated advocates, chartered accountants, and senior legal counsel under the Advocates Act, 1961.

1. Role as Data Fiduciary under Section 5 & 8

CraftEgon CCS ("Company", "we", "us") operates strictly as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. When you access our corporate intelligence tools, entity search directories, statutory fee calculators, or client onboarding workflows, we process digital personal data solely for specified lawful corporate secretarial, tax advisory, regulatory filing, and identity verification purposes.

2. Categories of Personal & Corporate Data Collected

In providing compliance automation and documentation infrastructure, we collect only necessary data categories:

  • Identity & KYC Records: Director Identification Numbers (DIN), Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), Aadhaar XML/hash (strictly for statutory verification via DigiLocker / authorized UIDAI gateways), and passport credentials;
  • Corporate Master Data: Corporate Identity Numbers (CIN), Limited Liability Partnership Identification Numbers (LLPIN), shareholding registers, cap tables, and board resolutions;
  • Financial & Tax Information: GSTIN registrations, bank account IFSC details for corporate account setup, and annual financial statements;
  • Digital Credentials: Class-3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) cryptographic tokens used strictly upon explicit client authorization for MCA V3 and Income Tax portal e-filings.

3. Rights of the Data Principal under Chapter III

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you, as a Data Principal, possess non-derogable statutory rights:

  1. Right to Access Information (Section 11): Right to obtain a summary of your personal data being processed and identities of Data Processors;
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Right to rectify inaccurate personal data or mandate complete erasure upon completion of statutory retention obligations;
  3. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Right to readily available grievance redressal mechanisms with guaranteed response windows within 7 business days;
  4. Right to Nominate (Section 14): Right to nominate an individual to exercise data principal rights in the event of death or incapacity.

4. Technical Security Safeguards & Data Retention

All records, intake submissions, and financial data are secured using industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Client data is stored within certified Tier-IV data centers located physically within the Republic of India. In accordance with Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Section 149 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, books of account, board minutes, and statutory registers are retained for a minimum of 8 financial years.
