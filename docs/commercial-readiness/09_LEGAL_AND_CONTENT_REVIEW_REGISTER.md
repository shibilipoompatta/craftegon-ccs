## 1. Governance Objectives

Corporate services platforms operate under intense regulatory scrutiny. Inaccurate statutory timelines, outdated government fees, incorrect penalty claims, or ambiguous legal demarcations can lead to client financial damages, consumer disputes, or disciplinary actions from professional institutes.

This register audits and verifies every key statutory assertion, legislative citation, government fee, and regulatory disclosure published across the CraftEgon Corporate Services website (`ccs.craftegon.com`).

## 3. Mandatory Reviewer Sign-Off Protocol

To maintain statutory accuracy over time, the following review schedule is established:
1. **Budget / Finance Act Review:** Annual review in July/August following the enactment of the Union Finance Act.
2. **MCA Notification Sweep:** Quarterly review of Ministry of Corporate Affairs circulars and Companies Rules amendments.
3. **Trademark & Patent Fee Verification:** Semi-annual verification against official IP India public notices.
4. **Sign-off Responsibility:** All statutory content updates require joint approval by the Head of Corporate Services Desk and an empaneled Practising Company Secretary.
