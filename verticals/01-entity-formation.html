Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What is the standard timeline and procedure for Private Limited Company incorporation under SPICe+ MCA V3? +
Incorporation is executed via the unified MCA V3 SPICe+ web-form (Part A for RUN name reservation, Part B for incorporation, DIN allocation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax, and bank account opening) typically completed in 3 to 5 business days upon digital signature verification.
What is the mandatory timeline for filing Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business)? +
Pursuant to Section 10A of the Companies Act, 2013, every company incorporated after November 2, 2018 having share capital must file e-Form INC-20A with the RoC within 180 days of incorporation confirming receipt of subscription money from directors.
