Vertical 04: Licenses & Statutory Registrations

Operational licensing support organized around the seven modules defined for this practice. Which approvals apply depends on the business activity, location, premises, workforce, and other case details; this page does not determine legal eligibility.

Seven operational modules

04.1 Municipal Establishments & Local Trade Licensing Desk

Site readiness, premises documentation, local licensing applications, and inspection coordination.

Source-listed outcomes: Shops & Commercial Establishments, municipal trade, and health trade licence records.

Related service: RoF maintenance & partnership registrations.

04.2 Social Security & Workforce Enrolment

Workforce-related enrolment support covering the source-defined EPFO, ESIC, and state professional tax scope.

Source-listed outcomes: EPF and ESIC establishment records, and PTEC/PTRC certificates where applicable.

Related service: PF / ESIC compliance.

04.3 MSME Udyam & Enterprise Enablement

Udyam registration support, activity classification, and preparation of the source-listed enterprise details.

Source-listed outcome: Udyam Registration Certificate & DPIIT Certificate of Recognition.

Related services: MSME / Udyam registration • DPIIT Startup India recognition.

04.4 Foreign Trade Architecture (DGFT IEC Desk)

DGFT profile and IEC application support, including the operational steps described in the source document.

Source-listed outcome: Import Export Code certificate.

Workflow: not yet implemented

04.5 FSSAI Food Safety Compliance Hub

Food-safety application support, category review, and related preparation described in the source document.

Source-listed outcomes: Basic Registration, State Licence, or Central Licence, as determined by verified case review.

Workflow: not yet implemented

04.6 Factory Licensing & Environmental Consents

Factory application and State Pollution Control Board consent support within the source-defined scope.

Source-listed outcomes: Factory licence and SPCB Consent to Establish / Consent to Operate records.

Workflow: not yet implemented

04.7 License Lifecycle Management & Renewal Desk

Lifecycle tracking and renewal filing support for relevant licences, subject to each authority's verified requirements.

Source-listed outcomes: Renewal receipts, updated validity records, and filing acknowledgements.

Tracking and reminder workflow: not yet implemented

Requirements require case-specific verification. The supplied specification contains incomplete or conflicting legal thresholds and fee details. No eligibility decision, government charge, service price, filing deadline, or renewal date is calculated or promised on this page.
