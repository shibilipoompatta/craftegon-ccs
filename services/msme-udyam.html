Who Needs Udyam & Statutory Classification Criteria

Udyam Registration is the sole official identification for MSMEs in India, replacing earlier certifications such as Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) and EM-II. All business entities engaged in manufacturing or service activities are eligible to obtain an Udyam Registration Number (URN).

Statutory Composite Criteria (Ministry Notification S.O. 2119(E)):

Enterprise Category Investment in Plant & Machinery / Equipment Annual Turnover Limit
Micro Enterprise Not exceeding ₹1 Crore Not exceeding ₹5 Crore
Small Enterprise Not exceeding ₹10 Crore Not exceeding ₹50 Crore
Medium Enterprise Not exceeding ₹50 Crore Not exceeding ₹250 Crore

Export Turnover Exemption: Under the MSMED Act statutory rules, exports of goods or services are strictly excluded from calculation of annual turnover for classification purposes.

Key Statutory & Commercial Benefits of Udyam

45-Day Payment Protections

Under Section 15 of MSMED Act & Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act, buyers must settle MSME dues within 45 days or face compounding interest and tax disallowances.

50% Trademark Fee Subsidy

Udyam certificate holders pay only ₹4,500 instead of ₹9,000 for government filing fees on Form TM-A trademark registrations.

Collateral-Free Loans (CGTMSE)

Eligible for priority sector lending from commercial banks and collateral-free credit facilities up to ₹5 Crore under CGTMSE.

Public Procurement Preferences

Exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) in public tenders and statutory price preference quotas on Government e-Marketplace (GeM).

Frequently Asked Questions (Udyam FAQs)

Is GSTIN mandatory to obtain an Udyam Registration?

GSTIN is mandatory for all enterprises that are statutorily required to register under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. However, micro enterprises that are legally exempt from GST registration (e.g. annual turnover below statutory threshold limits) can register on Udyam with PAN and Aadhaar only.

Does an Udyam Registration Certificate expire or require annual renewal?

No. The Udyam Registration Certificate has permanent lifetime validity and does not expire. However, enterprises must update their annual ITR and GST return details on the Udyam portal each year to maintain their official Micro, Small, or Medium classification status.

Can retail and wholesale traders register on Udyam?

Yes. The Ministry of MSME issued an official office memorandum allowing retail and wholesale traders (NIC codes 45, 46, and 47) to obtain Udyam Registration, specifically extending Priority Sector Lending (PSL) benefits from commercial banks.

How does Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act benefit MSME suppliers?

Under Section 43B(h), any buyer purchasing goods or services from a registered Micro or Small enterprise must clear their invoices within 15 days (or up to 45 days if agreed in writing). If delayed beyond this period, the buyer cannot claim the expense as a tax deduction until actual payment is made.

Can a business have more than one Udyam Registration Number?

No. Under Ministry regulations, only one URN can be issued per PAN. An enterprise operating multiple branches, manufacturing plants, or diverse business activities must add all additional units and NIC codes under its single master Udyam registration.
