Who Must File & Statutory Non-Compliance Penalties

Every individual who holds an active Director Identification Number (DIN) or Designated Partner Identification Number (DPIN) allocated on or before 31st March of the preceding financial year must submit their annual KYC to the Central Government by 30th September annually.

Critical Statutory Consequences of Non-Filing:

  • DIN Deactivation: On 1st October, the MCA portal automatically deactivates all unverified DINs with the status "Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC".
  • Company Filing Freeze: A company with a deactivated director cannot sign or submit ANY statutory RoC forms (including annual financials Form AOC-4, annual return MGT-7, auditor appointment ADT-1, or director changes DIR-12).
  • Mandatory ₹5,000 Statutory Penalty: To reactivate a deactivated DIN, the director must pay a mandatory statutory government penalty challan of ₹5,000 per DIN to the MCA Treasury.
  • Disqualification Risk: Prolonged non-compliance damages director credibility and risks disqualification scrutiny under Section 164(2) of the Companies Act.

Frequently Asked Questions (DIR-3 KYC FAQs)

What is the statutory annual deadline for filing DIR-3 KYC?

Under MCA regulations, DIR-3 KYC must be filed on or before 30th September of each financial year for every individual holding an approved DIN as of 31st March. Filings on or before 30th September have a ₹0 government fee.

What happens if I miss the 30th September deadline?

On 1st October, your DIN will be automatically deactivated on the MCA portal. You will be barred from digitally signing or submitting any MCA e-forms for any company. To reactivate your DIN, you must file the KYC along with a mandatory statutory government penalty of ₹5,000.

Do disqualified directors or directors of dormant companies still need to file?

Yes. The requirement applies to every DIN holder regardless of whether they are active, disqualified, or associated with a company. Even if your company is dormant or defunct, DIR-3 KYC must be filed to prevent permanent deactivation.

Can a foreign national holding a DIN file DIR-3 KYC with an international phone number?

Yes. The MCA V3 system allows foreign mobile country codes for verified foreign nationals holding DIN, enabling international OTP delivery during the verification process.

How do I know if I should file Web KYC or the e-Form?

If you filed DIR-3 KYC in the previous financial year and your mobile number, email, and personal details have not changed, you can file the simple Web-based KYC via OTP. If you are filing for the first time or updating your contact details or address, you must file the e-Form with DSC and CS certification.
