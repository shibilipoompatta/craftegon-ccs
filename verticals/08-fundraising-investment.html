Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What filings are mandatory with the RoC after closing a venture private placement? +
The company must file Form PAS-3 (Return of Allotment) within 15 days of share/CCPS allotment under Section 42(8) of the Companies Act 2013, Form MGT-14 for special resolutions within 30 days under Section 117, and complete stamp duty payment on share certificates within 60 days.
What is the statutory status of Angel Tax under Section 56(2)(viib) for Indian startups? +
Angel Tax under Section 56(2)(viib) of the Income Tax Act was legislatively abolished by the Finance Act 2024 for all classes of investors (both resident and non-resident). Indian startups and growing companies are no longer subjected to tax scrutiny on share premium received above fair market value, removing valuation friction during angel and venture rounds.
