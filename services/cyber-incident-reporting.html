Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

All service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates, and digital businesses operating ICT systems in India experiencing ransomware, DDoS, unauthorized data access, or system compromise.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • ICT system architecture diagram and server hosting topology (indicating Indian server footprint)
  • Incident telemetry data: compromised IP addresses, timestamped error logs, indicators of compromise (IoC)
  • System log architecture verifying 180-day rolling log retention within Indian jurisdiction
  • Network time synchronization verification with NTP servers of National Physical Laboratory (NPL)
  • Internal Incident Response Plan (IRP) and designated Point of Contact (PoC) registered with CERT-In
  • Technical root-cause analysis (RCA) and containment measures initiated

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Detect and triage incident against CERT-In 20 mandatory reportable cyber incident categories
  2. Step 2: Collect forensic telemetry, isolate affected systems, and notify internal breach response team
  3. Step 3: Compile Initial Incident Report in prescribed CERT-In format (Form Annexure-1)
  4. Step 4: Transmit initial report to CERT-In Incident Desk (`incident@cert-in.org.in`) within the strict 6-hour statutory window
  5. Step 5: Maintain and preserve 180-day forensic server logs within India for government regulatory inspection
  6. Step 6: Submit Final Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and mitigation report to CERT-In within statutory follow-up timelines

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to report cyber incidents within 6 hours or non-compliance with CERT-In directions under Section 70B(7) attracts imprisonment up to 1 year or a fine up to ₹1,00,000, or both, for responsible corporate officers. Exposes company to uncapped compensation claims under Section 43A.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the mandatory timeline for reporting a cybersecurity incident to CERT-In?
Cyber incidents must be formally reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing the incident or being brought to notice, as mandated by the 2022 CERT-In directions.
What are the requirements for system log retention under Indian law?
All corporate entities and service providers must enable and securely maintain ICT system logs within the Indian jurisdiction for a rolling period of 180 days.
