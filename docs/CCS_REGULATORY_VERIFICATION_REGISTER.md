## 1. Executive Purpose & Legal Verification Standards

This register performs a rigorous, line-by-line statutory audit of all legal, regulatory, and procedural claims published across CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) web assets, marketing materials, and internal blueprints.

### Statutory Audit Standard:
Under no circumstances may CCS publish unverified statutory claims, outdated provisions, or mischaracterizations of professional boundaries. Every claim is classified into five mutually exclusive legal categories:
1. `VERIFIED`: Confirmed against current primary statutory acts, rules, and gazette notifications.
2. `REQUIRES_CURRENT_VERIFICATION`: Enacted primary statute with pending subordinate rules or circulars.
3. `REQUIRES_PROFESSIONAL_INTERPRETATION`: Complex statutory provisions subject to judicial precedent or multi-agency interpretation.
4. `INCORRECT_OR_MISLEADING`: Factually inaccurate, repealed, or legally mischaracterized claims requiring immediate redaction.
5. `NOT_SUITABLE_FOR_PUBLIC_PUBLICATION`: Proprietary internal benchmarks or speculative advice not suitable for external publication.

## 3. Critical Statutory Redactions & Mandatory Corrections

### Finding 1: Legislative Abolition of Section 56(2)(viib) "Angel Tax" (Finance Act 2024)
- **Issue:** Service `V01-SVC-001` and related marketing materials promote DPIIT recognition as providing exemption from "Angel Tax under Section 56(2)(viib)".
- **Statutory Reality:** The Finance Act, 2024 officially abolished Section 56(2)(viib) for all classes of investors effective Assessment Year 2025-26.
- **Mandatory Correction:** Immediately update `services/dpiit-startup-india.html` and related collateral. Pivot the DPIIT value proposition to Section 80-IAC tax holidays, public procurement exemptions, and 80% intellectual property fee rebates.

### Finding 2: Direct Legal Representation Disclaimer (Advocates Act 1961)
- **Issue:** Several conversion and service pages historical language implied "in-house dispute representation".
- **Statutory Reality:** Under Section 29 and Section 33 of the Advocates Act 1961, corporate entities cannot practice law or represent parties in courts/tribunals.
- **Mandatory Correction:** Strictly maintain the 3 referral pages as pure external referral channels, with explicit disclaimers that advocates act independently under direct client vakalatnamas.

### Finding 3: Separation of Direct Coordination vs Reserved CA/CS Statutory Acts
- **Issue:** Public perception that CraftEgon CCS performs statutory audits or secretarial certifications as a company.
- **Statutory Reality:** Statutory audits under Section 139 and Secretarial Audits under Section 204 can strictly be signed by individual practicing CAs and CSs holding active Certificates of Practice and personal UDINs.
- **Mandatory Correction:** Ensure all service proposals, invoices, and website disclosures state that CraftEgon acts as the coordination and documentation partner, while statutory certifications are executed by accredited independent professional partners.
