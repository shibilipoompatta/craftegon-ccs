Section 241 & 242 Oppression and Mismanagement Statutory Guide

Statutory guide on shareholder remedies against mismanagement, fund diversion, asset stripping, and illegal share dilution under Sections 241–242 of the Companies Act, 2013. CraftEgon CCS provides secretarial and accounting forensic records compilation; formal NCLT representation is conducted exclusively by independent legal counsel.

Statutory Notice & Legal Framework: CraftEgon CCS provides corporate secretarial, compliance, and document drafting infrastructure. Independent Legal Counsel Representation

CraftEgon CCS assists enterprises and stakeholders with secretarial audits, statutory register reconstructions, and forensic accounting documentation only. Under the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India regulations, corporate compliance advisory entities do not enter appearances, plead, or provide contentious tribunal advocacy.

Shareholders or respondents involved in Section 241 or Section 242 company petitions before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) must engage an independent practicing advocate for petition drafting, filing, and hearing appearances.

Referral Notice: CraftEgon CCS maintains no in-house legal advocacy service, advocate panel agreement, or exclusive referral destination in this repository. External representation must be retained independently through counsel of the client's choice.
