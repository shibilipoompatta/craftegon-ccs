Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

An LLP is the preferred statutory structure for service businesses, professional advisors (engineers, architects, consultants, creative agencies), and joint ventures that desire limited liability without the rigid secretarial burden, mandatory statutory audits, and board meeting rituals of a Private Limited Company.

Mandatory Statutory Eligibility Requirements under the LLP Act, 2008:

  • Minimum Partners: At least 2 partners (individuals or corporate bodies). No upper limit on the maximum number of partners under Section 5.
  • Designated Partners (DPIN): At least 2 individual partners must act as "Designated Partners", responsible for statutory filings and legal compliance.
  • Indian Resident Partner: Under Section 7(1), at least one Designated Partner must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for ≥ 182 days during the financial year.
  • Capital Contribution: No minimum capital threshold required by law. Partners can contribute tangible or intangible property, cash, or agreements to contribute.
  • Registered Office: A physical premises address within India where statutory communications can be received.

Scope of Work & Exclusions

Included in Scope

Statutory Inclusions:

  • Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) with USB encryption tokens for 2 Designated Partners.
  • Designated Partner Identification Number (DPIN) application for up to 2 partners via FiLLiP.
  • Name reservation via RUN-LLP or integrated FiLLiP Part A with phonetic clearance.
  • Preparation of FiLLiP incorporation form and compilation of partner consents (Form 9).
  • Custom drafting of the comprehensive LLP Agreement detailing profit-sharing, voting rights, and exit mechanisms.
  • Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) generation.
  • Certificate of Incorporation (COI) issued by the Registrar of Companies with LLPIN.
  • Drafting and filing of mandatory Form 3 (LLP Agreement) within 30 days of incorporation.
Ongoing & Excluded Compliances

Exclusions & Subsequent Obligations:

  • State Stamp Duty on LLP Agreement: State stamp duty varies by state (e.g. ₹1,000–₹5,000) and is paid directly to the State Revenue Treasury at actuals.
  • Form 11 Annual Return: Mandatory annual filing due within 60 days of FY closure (May 30) (available under separate secretarial mandate).
  • Form 8 Statement of Account & Solvency: Mandatory solvency filing due within 30 days of half-year end (October 30).
  • Statutory Tax Audit: Tax audit under Section 44AB if turnover exceeds statutory limits.
  • Trademark / Brand Protection: Form TM-A registration must be engaged separately under IPR practice.

Frequently Asked Questions (LLP FAQs)

Can a salaried individual be a Designated Partner in an LLP?

Under the LLP Act, there is no statutory prohibition against salaried individuals becoming partners. However, the individual must review their existing employment agreement to ensure there is no dual employment or non-compete clause restricting commercial directorships or partnerships.

Is a statutory audit mandatory for all LLPs in India?

No. Under Section 34(4) of the LLP Act and Rule 24 of LLP Rules, an LLP is exempt from mandatory statutory audit if its annual turnover does not exceed ₹40 Lakhs and its total capital contribution does not exceed ₹25 Lakhs. Once either threshold is crossed, a Chartered Accountant audit is mandatory.

Can an LLP be converted into a Private Limited Company later?

Yes. An LLP can convert into a Private Limited Company under Section 366 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Companies (Authorized to Register) Rules, 2014, allowing businesses to transition once they are ready to raise institutional venture capital.

Can a residential address be registered as the LLP's official office?

Yes. A residential property owned or rented by any of the partners can serve as the registered office of the LLP, provided a recent electricity/utility bill and an authorized No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner are submitted.

What happens if Form 3 is not filed within 30 days of incorporation?

Under Section 69 of the LLP Act, delayed filing of Form 3 attracts compounding statutory late fees of ₹100 per day without an upper ceiling. CraftEgon CCS prioritizes Form 3 drafting and execution concurrently with FiLLiP approval to prevent penalties.
