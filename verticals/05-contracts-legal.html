Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What are essential statutory clauses in an Indian Founder Shareholder Agreement (SHA)? +
A court-tested SHA under the Indian Contract Act 1872 and Companies Act 2013 must include clear Reverse Vesting schedules (typically 4-year with 1-year cliff), Right of First Refusal (ROFR), Tag-Along and Drag-Along rights, Reserved Matters (Super-majority voting), and Liquidation Preference cascades.
How can an arbitration clause avoid Indian court delays? +
By stipulating institutional arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (such as MCIA or SIAC rules), specifying an exclusive legal seat (e.g., Bengaluru or Mumbai), and restricting emergency relief jurisdictions.
