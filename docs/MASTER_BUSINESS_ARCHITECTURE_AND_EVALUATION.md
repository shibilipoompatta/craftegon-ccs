# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CRAFTEGON Corporate Services (CCS) is intended to operate as an integrated corporate services and business infrastructure platform supporting entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs, established companies and other organisations throughout their business lifecycle.

CCS is not intended to be limited to company registration, tax filing or isolated compliance assignments.

Its strategic purpose is to help businesses:

1. Establish the right legal and operational structure.
2. Obtain the registrations, licences and approvals required for their activities.
3. Maintain corporate, tax and statutory compliance.
4. Protect their commercial interests, contracts, intellectual property and information.
5. Manage workforce, governance and operational risks.
6. Access specialist support when raising capital, restructuring or expanding.
7. Maintain ongoing corporate administration and business infrastructure.

The business will be organised through four connected architectural layers:

| Layer                         | Function                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer-facing portfolio     | Six clear core service categories on the website                      |
| Internal service architecture | Fifteen specialist business verticals                                 |
| Strategic business framework  | Five broad Start, Comply, Protect, Scale and Digital pillars          |
| Delivery and revenue model    | Project services, recurring compliance, advisory and digital services |

The intended long-term position is:

**CRAFTEGON CCS – Your Business Infrastructure & Corporate Services Partner.**

The business should launch with a focused set of commercially viable services and progressively expand its specialist capabilities without compromising professional standards, service quality or operational control.

# CRAFTEGON CCS WEBSITE — CRITICAL EVALUATION

**Scope:** Evaluation of the codebase as an integrated service platform.

## 1. Executive Assessment
**Overall Assessment:** Promising foundation; material launch risks.

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Brand and positioning | Good foundation; needs sharper differentiation |
| Navigation and service architecture | Strong (15 verticals, 6 core categories) |
| Visual and mobile experience | Requires browser-level validation |
| Conversion and lead capture | Incomplete in production mode |
| Pricing governance | Good internal controls, public-build defect identified |
| Legal/regulatory accuracy | High-risk; requires expert verification |
| Security and privacy | Thoughtful controls, but not production-proven |
| Backend and portal | Not operationally established |
| SEO and content | Broad coverage; quality and authority need strengthening |
| Deployment readiness | Build succeeds; release validation incomplete |

---

## 2. Strengths Identified
1. **Mature Business Architecture:** 57 service pages, 15 vertical hubs, knowledge centre, conversion desks, structured datasets.
2. **Professional & Regulatory Boundaries:** Explicit distinction of litigation referrals from direct services; recognition of CS/CA qualified practitioner boundaries.
3. **Release Architecture Safeguards:** Automatic exclusion of `/admin/`, `/portal/`, `/src/`, and `/docs/` from public builds; manifest generation; link sanitisation.
4. **Automated Testing Suite:** Tests for links, orphan pages, SEO, schemas, search, chat routing, forms, and pricing.

---

## 3. Critical Defects & Exposure Points
1. **Critical: Unapproved Prices in Public Output:**
   - Commercial governance framework mandates unapproved prices must not be published.
   - Build script price masking must not rely solely on CSS classes; structured commercial content controls required.
2. **Critical: Production Website Intake Limitations:**
   - Form handler defaults to WhatsApp/email fallback in static mode; central lead-management backend with database persistence, notifications, and conversion tracking needed for enterprise scaling.
3. **High: Backend & Portal Operational Readiness:**
   - Backend code exists, but secured, deployed production operations (cloud DB, live payments, auth portal) must not be marketed until fully provisioned and acceptance-tested.
4. **High: Regulatory Content Maintenance:**
   - Statutory penalties, thresholds, deadlines, and state-specific stamp duty require dated sources and qualified CS/CA verification registers.
5. **High: Homepage Proposition:**
   - Must lead with customer outcome (business setup, ongoing compliance, corporate infrastructure) rather than overwhelming with raw directory listings.
6. **High: Avoiding Feature Overstatement:**
   - Distinguish available services from manual coordination, independent professional delivery, or planned features.
7. **Medium: Security Assessment:**
   - Access control testing, signed URL expirations, file upload quarantine, and tight API CORS policies required before opening authenticated API endpoints.
