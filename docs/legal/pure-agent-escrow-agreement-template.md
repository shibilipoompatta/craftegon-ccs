# CRAFTEGON LLP: Client Pure Agent Escrow Authorization Agreement
**Statutory Framework:** Rule 33 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Rules, 2017 & Section 15 of the CGST Act, 2017.

---

## 1. Parties & Purpose
This Pure Agent Authorization & Escrow Agreement is entered into between:
1. **CRAFTEGON LLP** (LLPIN: [LLPIN]), an Indian Limited Liability Partnership having its principal corporate place of business at Kozhikode, Kerala ("**Platform Facilitator**" or "**Pure Agent**"); and
2. **The Client** (as designated in the onboarding authorization form or service intake booking) ("**Principal Recipient**").

Whereas the Principal Recipient engages the Platform Facilitator to coordinate corporate incorporation, intellectual property registration, or statutory compliance filings before official regulatory authorities including the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the Trade Marks Registry (CGPDTM), the Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN), and State Stamp Duty Treasuries.

---

## 2. Statutory Conditions of Pure Agent Engagement (Rule 33 CGST Rules)
In accordance with Rule 33 of the CGST Rules, 2017, the expenditure incurred by CRAFTEGON LLP on behalf of the Principal Recipient shall be excluded from the value of supply for GST calculation, subject to strict adherence to the following conditions:

1. **Authorization:** The Platform Facilitator acts as a pure agent of the Principal Recipient when making payments to third parties/government bodies for statutory challans and stamp duty.
2. **Direct Liability:** The statutory fee liability (e.g., MCA SPICe+ Part B, FiLLiP filing fees, Name Reservation RUN fees, Trademark TM-A filing fees, Bharatkosh payments, State Stamp Duty) is legally the obligation of the Principal Recipient.
3. **Zero Markup / Pass-Through:** The Platform Facilitator incurs these costs strictly on an actual pass-through basis. Neither markup, commission, interest, nor premium is added to official government receipts.
4. **Third-Party Receipts:** Official challans, payment acknowledgments, and receipt numbers (e.g., MCA SRN receipt, Bharatkosh Transaction ID, IP India CBR receipt) are issued in the legal name of the entity/applicant and handed over directly to the Principal Recipient.
5. **Separation of Consideration:** In all invoices issued by CRAFTEGON LLP, professional consulting fees are distinctly separated from Pure Agent statutory disbursements:
   * **Part A (Professional Services):** Taxable at applicable GST rate (18% SAC 9983).
   * **Part B (Pure Agent Disbursements):** Nil GST under Rule 33 of CGST Rules, 2017.

---

## 3. Dedicated Escrow Account Mechanics
All estimated statutory disbursements collected from the Principal Recipient shall be deposited into a segregated, non-interest-bearing bank account:
* **Account Name:** `CRAFTEGON LLP - Client Statutory Escrow A/c`
* **Bank:** [Designated Scheduled Commercial Bank, Kozhikode Branch]
* **Purpose:** Dedicated exclusively to official government challan generation and treasury disbursements.
* **Audit Trail:** Every outflow from this account must correspond 1:1 with an official government challan SRN/CBR receipt.

---

## 4. Reconciliation, Shortfalls & Refunds
1. **State Stamp Duty Fluctuations:** State stamp duties vary across states (e.g., Kerala vs. Karnataka vs. Maharashtra) and depend upon authorized capital thresholds. If actual state stamp duty differs from initial estimates, the Platform Facilitator shall issue an itemized reconciliation statement.
2. **Unused Balances:** Any unspent balance in the escrow ledger following the completion of statutory filings shall be refunded to the client's source bank account within three (3) business days.
3. **Challan Expirations:** If a statutory challan expires due to client delay in executing signatures or providing required KYC, any late fees or re-filing charges levied by the regulatory portal shall be borne solely by the Principal Recipient.

The Principal Recipient indemnifies CRAFTEGON LLP, its designated partners, and empanelled professionals against any legal consequence, penalty, or investigation arising from fabricated or misleading information submitted by the client.
