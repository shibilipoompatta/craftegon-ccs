Statutory Mandate: Section 139(1) read with Sections 44AD, 44ADA, and 44AB of the Income-tax Act, 1961

Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Sole proprietorships, trade professionals, freelancers, and SMEs. ITR-4 applies to turnover up to ₹3 Crores (under Section 44AD for business) or ₹75 Lakhs (under Section 44ADA for specified professionals). ITR-3 applies to all proprietary businesses maintaining books of accounts.

Prerequisites & Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, and bank account statements of proprietor for all operational accounts
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS) extracts
  • Form 26AS verifying TDS deductions under 194C, 194J, 194H, and 194Q
  • Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet (or gross receipts & cash transactions for ITR-4)
  • Depreciation schedules under Section 32 and business expense ledgers
  • Tax Audit Report in Form 3CA/3CB-3CD (if turnover exceeds Section 44AB thresholds)

Step-by-Step Portal Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1: Download AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS from Income Tax Portal; reconcile with bank ledgers
  2. Step 2: Determine appropriate regime: Old Tax Regime (with deductions) vs New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)
  3. Step 3: Select appropriate form (ITR-4 Sugam for presumptive or ITR-3 for detailed P&L accounting)
  4. Step 4: Populate gross receipts, business expenditures, balance sheet figures, and Chapter VI-A deductions
  5. Step 5: Pre-validate tax calculation, advance tax credits, and self-assessment tax dues
  6. Step 6: Submit return; verify via Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or DSC; download ITR-V acknowledgement

Government Fees & Disbursements

Government Statutory Fee: ₹0 (NIL) if filed within statutory due date (31st July for non-audit cases, 31st October for audit cases).

Statutory Penalties for Non-Compliance

Late filing attracts late fee under Section 234F (₹5,000, or ₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5 Lakhs) plus 1% per month interest under Section 234A/B/C. Late filing permanently forfeits carry-forward of business losses and unabsorbed depreciation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the benefit of presumptive taxation under Section 44AD?
Eligible businesses can declare net profit at 8% (or 6% for digital turnover) without maintaining cumbersome books of accounts or undergoing tax audit.
Can a proprietor switch between the old and new tax regimes?
Individuals with business income can opt out of the default new tax regime only once by filing Form 10-IEA; switching back later is strictly limited.
