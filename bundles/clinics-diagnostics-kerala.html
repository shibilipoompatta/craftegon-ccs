Step 1 • Kerala Market Reality

Why Standard Registration Fails After Day 1 in Kerala

Kerala Demand Dynamics

High-Intent Capital & Sector Expansion

Kozhikode, Malappuram, and the Malabar-Kochi corridor are seeing rapid capital inflows from GCC-return doctors and healthcare entrepreneurs establishing branded clinics, diagnostic centers, and specialized care/research facilities requiring institutional governance from day one.

Where Fragmented Vendors Fail

The Post-Registration Operational Stall

General incorporation portals stop after issuing a Certificate of Incorporation, leaving clinic promoters stranded for months navigating LSGD trade/building usage rules, Kerala Clinical Establishments registration standards (beyond single-doctor consultation thresholds), KSPCB Bio-Medical Waste (BMW) authorizations, and unstructured handshake arrangements with visiting specialists.

Government filing fees, Kerala Stamp Duty, KSPCB consent fees, and third-party inspection or CBMWTF subscription costs are payable at actuals and strictly separated from CCS advisory fees. You receive an itemised scope separation before work begins.
