Who This Service Is For & Statutory Eligibility

Trademark registration is vital for any enterprise investing capital and effort into building brand equity, customer goodwill, or intellectual property. Without a registered mark, competitors can copy your product names, duplicate your logos, or register identical domain names without statutory infringement penalties.

Applicant Categories & Concessional Fee Eligibility:

  • Individual / Sole Proprietor: Eligible for the lower statutory government filing fee of ₹4,500 per class.
  • Startups (DPIIT Recognized): Entities holding a valid DPIIT Certificate of Recognition receive an official 50% concession, paying ₹4,500 per class.
  • MSMEs (Udyam Registered): Enterprises possessing an official Udyam Registration Certificate pay ₹4,500 per class (50% statutory discount).
  • Other Corporate Bodies: Companies, LLPs, or partnerships without MSME/Startup status pay the standard statutory fee of ₹9,000 per class.

Frequently Asked Questions (Trademark FAQs)

When can I legally start using the ™ symbol?

You can start using the ™ symbol immediately upon filing Form TM-A and receiving the official Trademark Application Number from the Registry. The ® symbol can ONLY be used after the Registry issues the final Certificate of Registration.

How long does it take to get a final Trademark Registration Certificate?

While the ™ rights and application number are obtained within 24–48 hours, the complete statutory process—including examination by the Registrar, advertisement in the official Journal, and the mandatory 4-month public opposition period—typically takes 6 to 12 months.

Should I apply for a Wordmark or a Logo (Device Mark)?

A Wordmark provides the broadest legal protection because it protects the brand name in any style, font, size, or color. A Device Mark protects a specific graphic logo design or stylized artwork. We generally recommend filing a Wordmark first, followed by a Device Mark for key brand symbols.

How long does a trademark registration remain valid in India?

Under Section 25 of the Trade Marks Act, a registered trademark is valid for 10 years from the application filing date. It can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years by filing Form TM-R with the statutory renewal fee.

How does MSME Udyam registration save 50% on government trademark fees?

Under the Trade Marks Rules 2017, the Government of India provides a 50% statutory fee waiver for small enterprises. By attaching a valid Udyam certificate, the statutory filing fee is reduced from ₹9,000 to ₹4,500 per class, creating immediate cost savings.
