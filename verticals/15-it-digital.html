Statutory Q&A Authoritative Legal Provisions & FAQs

What are the core obligations of a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023? +
Under Sections 5 and 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, every Data Fiduciary must give itemized notice in clear and plain language (in English and 22 Eighth Schedule languages) stating specific purposes, personal data categories, grievance redressal mechanisms, and obtain verifiable, unconditional consent before processing.
What is the statutory penalty for personal data breach under the DPDP Act 2023? +
The Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) can levy financial penalties up to ₹250 Crores for failure to observe reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches, and up to ₹200 Crores for failure to notify the Board and affected Data Principals.
