Diagnostic Lookup vs Passive Extracts

Structured Registry Screening & Remediation Readiness

Public company directories often leave founders with raw registry tables without action steps. CraftEgon CCS connects entity, trademark, and GSTIN checks directly to statutory deadline context and scoping support.

Evaluation Dimension Passive Registry Listings CraftEgon CCS Registry Screen
Reading & Clarity Ad-heavy pages with unformatted tables and minimal mobile adaptation Ad-free, responsive layout with structured company, LLP, trademark & GSTIN panels
Regulatory Context Raw status flags without explaining Section 403 or Rule 12A impact Links overdue status directly to Section 403 fee estimators & compliance calculators
Remediation Path No operational path from lookup results to filing preparation Direct handoff to AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC, STK-2, and CS/CA scoping desks
Summary Output Scattered screens across multiple tabs and portals Printable due-diligence readiness summary with itemized document checklist
Official Source Links Manual portal copy-paste across MCA, IP India, and GSTN Quick query copy & official portal links (MCA V3, IP India Trade Marks, GSTN)
