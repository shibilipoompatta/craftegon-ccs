DIR-3 KYC and Director Particulars Update

DIR-3 KYC is governed by Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014. The current framework generally requires KYC once in every third consecutive financial year, by 30 June following that cycle. Changes to mobile number, email address or residential address must be intimated within 30 days. Check current MCA records and applicable rules before filing.

Statutory Authority

Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)

Mandatory Scope

All Approved DIN / DPIN Holders

Indicative turnaround

Depends on portal, OTP and document readiness

Who Must File & KYC Compliance Considerations

Under the amended Rule 12A, a DIN holder generally submits KYC particulars once in every third consecutive financial year, by 30 June following the end of that third financial year. This is not an annual 30 September filing cycle. Applicability and due date should be checked against the DIN holder’s last filing and current MCA records.

Important compliance considerations:

  • Missed KYC cycle: A DIN may be marked deactivated for non-filing in accordance with applicable MCA rules and portal processes. Verify the live DIN status before advising on remediation.
  • Change reporting: Changes to mobile number, email address or residential address are subject to the prescribed 30-day intimation requirement.
  • Fees and reactivation: Any additional fee or reactivation requirement must be confirmed from current MCA rules and the portal challan for the specific case.
  • Filing impact: Check the actual DIN status and signing-authority position before assessing any impact on company filings.

What You Provide (Director Input Checklist)

To process your DIR-3 KYC within hours, our desk requires the following basic inputs:

1. 8-Digit DIN Number

Your allocated Director Identification Number or DPIN as recorded in MCA records.

2. Live OTP Availability

Availability during filing to share the 2 separate OTPs delivered to your registered mobile and email within the 15-minute portal window.

3. Self-Attested KYC (e-Form Only)

If updating details: self-attested PAN card, address proof (under 2 months old), and valid passport (for foreign nationals).

Required Documents Checklist

For Web-Based KYC (Unchanged Details):

  • 8-digit approved DIN / DPIN.
  • Active mobile number registered in previous filing.
  • Active personal email ID registered in previous filing.
  • Zero physical documentation required.

For e-Form KYC (First-Time / Updating Details):

  • Self-attested PAN card copy.
  • Self-attested Passport / Voter ID / Driving License.
  • Recent personal bank statement / electricity bill (not older than 2 months).
  • Valid Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).

Mandatory Statutory Professional Certification (Rule 12A)

Under Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014, every DIR-3 KYC e-Form must be verified and certified by a practicing professional - either an Associate/Fellow Company Secretary (ACS/FCS), Chartered Accountant (ACA/FCA), or Cost Accountant (ACMA/FCMA). Frequently Asked Questions (DIR-3 KYC FAQs)

What is the current DIR-3 KYC filing cycle?

Under amended Rule 12A, KYC is generally required once in every third consecutive financial year, by 30 June following that third financial year. Check the individual’s last KYC filing and current MCA record to calculate the next due date.

What if I miss the applicable KYC due date?

The DIN status and remedial filing route should be checked on the MCA portal. Any additional fee or reactivation step depends on applicable rules and the individual record; confirm applicable fees from the current MCA portal.

Do disqualified directors or directors of dormant companies still need to file?

Applicability depends on the DIN holder’s status, filing history and current MCA records. A dormant or defunct company does not by itself determine the individual’s next KYC due date; check the applicable Rule 12A cycle and portal status.

Can a foreign national holding a DIN file DIR-3 KYC with an international phone number?

Yes. The MCA V3 system allows foreign mobile country codes for verified foreign nationals holding DIN, enabling international OTP delivery during the verification process.

How do I know if I should file Web KYC or the e-Form?

The MCA portal and current filing instructions determine whether Web KYC or the e-Form route applies. Do not infer the filing mode solely from a prior-year submission. Changes to prescribed particulars must be intimated within the applicable 30-day period.
