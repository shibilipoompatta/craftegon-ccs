Who Needs Udyam & Statutory Classification Criteria

Udyam Registration is the sole official identification for MSMEs in India, replacing earlier certifications such as Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) and EM-II. All business entities engaged in manufacturing or service activities are eligible to obtain an Udyam Registration Number (URN).

Statutory Composite Criteria (Ministry Notification S.O. 2119(E)):

Enterprise Category Investment in Plant & Machinery / Equipment Annual Turnover Limit
Micro Enterprise Not exceeding ₹1 Crore Not exceeding ₹5 Crore
Small Enterprise Not exceeding ₹10 Crore Not exceeding ₹50 Crore
Medium Enterprise Not exceeding ₹50 Crore Not exceeding ₹250 Crore

Export Turnover Exemption: Under the MSMED Act statutory rules, exports of goods or services are strictly excluded from calculation of annual turnover for classification purposes.

Exclusions & Separate Mandates

Statutory Exclusions:

  • Loan Sanctions & Subsidies: Bank credit approvals and government subsidies are subject to independent commercial bank and scheme underwriting criteria.
  • MSEFC Contentious Litigation: Filing recovery petitions or dispute advocacy before the Micro and Small Enterprise Facilitation Council under Section 18 requires independent advocate representation under the Advocates Act, 1961.
  • Local Trade & Police Licenses: Municipal trade licenses, FSSAI registrations, and pollution clearances are separate statutory mandates.
  • Annual GST / RoC Filings: Routine periodic tax returns and corporate ROC secretarial compliances must be engaged separately.

Required Information & Documents Checklist

1. Applicant & Entity Identification

  • Aadhaar Card of the authorized signatory (linked with active mobile for OTP).
  • PAN Card of the business entity (or proprietor's PAN for proprietorships).
  • GSTIN (GST Identification Number), if registered or mandatory.
  • Entity Incorporation Certificate / Partnership Deed (if applicable).

2. Plant Location & Banking Data

  • Official enterprise address with PIN code and district.
  • Location of plant, warehouse, or service delivery units.
  • Active business bank account number and IFSC code.
  • Date of commencement of commercial operations.

3. Operational & Financial Figures

  • Total number of employees (Male / Female / Other).
  • Written Down Value (WDV) of Plant & Machinery from latest ITR.
  • Total annual turnover & export turnover as declared in ITR/GST.
  • Specific business activities for 5-digit NIC code assignment.
