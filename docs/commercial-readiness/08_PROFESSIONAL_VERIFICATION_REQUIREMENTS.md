## 1. Governance Objectives

CraftEgon Corporate Services (CCS) operates a hybrid service delivery model. While direct administrative tasks are executed by internal staff, statutory corporate certifications and filings are legally entrusted to independent, licensed professionals.

To protect client interests, mitigate liability, and comply with the statutory guidelines of the **Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI)**, the **Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)**, the **Office of the CGPDTM (IP India)**, and the **Bar Council of India (BCI)**, this framework establishes mandatory verification standards for all empaneled professionals.

No professional may certify documents, access client data, or execute filings under the CraftEgon network without completing this verification protocol.

## 2. Professional Credential Verification Standards

### A. Practising Company Secretaries (PCS) — ICSI
- **Statutory Authority:** The Company Secretaries Act, 1980 & Companies Act, 2013 (Section 2(51), 7(1)(b), 204, etc.).
- **Mandatory Credentials Checked:**
  1. **Associate / Fellow Membership Certificate (ACS / FCS):** Verified directly against the ICSI Online Member Directory.
  2. **Active Certificate of Practice (CoP):** Verified as current and in force (annual fee paid; not surrendered or suspended).
  3. **Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN):** Verified that the PCS generates and attaches a valid ICSI UDIN to every statutory certification.
  4. **Peer Review Certificate:** Mandatory for PCS undertaking Secretarial Audits for listed or high-turnover entities under ICSI guidelines.
  5. **Active MCA V3 Portal Registration:** Verified that the professional's Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) is registered and mapped under the "Professional" category on the MCA V3 portal.

### B. Chartered Accountants in Practice (CA) — ICAI
- **Statutory Authority:** The Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 & Companies Act, 2013 (Section 139–147).
- **Mandatory Credentials Checked:**
  1. **ACA / FCA Membership Number:** Verified against the ICAI Member Portal.
  2. **Active Certificate of Practice (CoP):** Confirmed in full-time practice.
  3. **Firm Registration Number (FRN):** If operating through a partnership or LLP, the firm constitution and active status are verified.
  4. **ICAI UDIN Compliance:** All tax audit reports (Form 3CA/3CB-3CD), financial certifications, and net worth certificates must bear a system-generated ICAI UDIN.
  5. **Income Tax & MCA Portal Mapping:** Active DSC registered on the Income Tax e-Filing and MCA V3 systems.

### C. Registered Trademark & Patent Agents — IP India
- **Statutory Authority:** The Trade Marks Act, 1999 (Section 145) & Trade Marks Rules, 2017.
- **Mandatory Credentials Checked:**
  1. **Trademark Agent Registration Number (TMA Code):** Verified on the official CGPDTM Registry of Trademark Agents.
  2. **Active Digital Signature Certificate:** Class 3 DSC validly mapped to the IP India e-Filing portal for Form TM-A submissions.
  3. **Good Standing:** Absence of disqualification or cancellation under Rule 152 of the Trade Marks Rules.

### D. Independent Legal Counsel (Advocate Panel) — Bar Council of India
- **Statutory Authority:** The Advocates Act, 1961 (Section 29, 30).
- **Mandatory Verification Checked:**
  1. **State Bar Council Enrollment Number:** Verified against the relevant State Bar Council roll (e.g. Bar Council of Kerala, Bar Council of Delhi).
  2. **All India Bar Examination (AIBE) Certificate:** Verified for advocates enrolled after 2010.
  3. **Advocates Act Non-Association Undertaking:** The advocate signs a declaration confirming they act in an independent individual or chamber capacity, and that CraftEgon does not exercise control over legal advice or retain advocacy fees.

## 3. Empanelment Document Checklist

Before any professional is assigned to client matters, the compliance desk must assemble and verify the following dossier:

```
[ ] 1. Copy of PAN Card & Aadhaar Card (Identity Verification)
[ ] 2. Copy of Professional Membership Certificate (ACS/FCS, ACA/FCA, or BCI Enrollment)
[ ] 3. Current Year Certificate of Practice (CoP) Renewal Receipt
[ ] 4. Test Verification of MCA V3 / Income Tax / IP India Portal Login
[ ] 5. Specimen Digital Signature Certificate (Class 3 Signing & Encryption)
[ ] 6. Executed Bilateral Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Fee Schedule
[ ] 7. Signed Non-Disclosure & DPDP Act Data Confidentiality Undertaking
[ ] 8. Annual Conflict of Interest Declaration
```

---

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Professional Conduct Obligations

Every empaneled CS, CA, or TM Agent agrees to the following contractual terms:
1. **Statutory Turnaround SLA:**
   - SPICe+ Part B certification: Execution within **24 business hours** of client document completion.
   - DIR-3 KYC certification: Execution within **4 business hours** of OTP verification.
   - Form TM-A submission: Filing and provisional receipt delivery within **24 business hours**.
2. **Quality & Zero Resubmission Standard:**
   - Professionals must perform rigorous preliminary scrutiny on name availability, registered office utility bills (not older than 2 months), and identity proofs to minimize MCA resubmission notices.
3. **Data Confidentiality & Non-Solicitation:**
   - Strict prohibition against soliciting CraftEgon clients directly outside the agreed engagement structure.
   - Mandatory deletion or secure encrypted archiving of client personal documents upon completion of statutory filings in compliance with the DPDP Act, 2023.

---

## 5. Annual Audit & Disciplinary Review

1. **Annual Audit Rhythm:** Every year in April, the compliance desk reviews all empaneled professionals to verify that membership dues and CoP renewals have been cleared.
2. **Disciplinary Watchlist Check:** Checks against ICSI and ICAI disciplinary committees to ensure no panelist has faced suspension or reprimand for professional misconduct.
3. **Removal & Blacklisting:** Immediate revocation of empanelment occurs if a professional fails to generate UDIN, causes statutory filing default due to unexcused delay, or compromises client confidentiality.
